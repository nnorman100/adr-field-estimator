# Access matrix: Jobs routes

Part of [Access matrix](../ACCESS.md).

## Jobs routes (rebuilt app, S5a-3)

`web/src/lib/jobs/`, five handlers under `/api/jobs`, all `memberRoute`; the company is the caller's own from the sign-in and no path, query or body names one. No new capability key: each route uses keys the table above already has.

| Route                         | Who                       | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `POST /api/jobs`              | `jobs_create`             | The phone sends the job id and the first draft id. The job row is written first, then its Firestore field draft; sent again, it makes whichever of the two is missing (201 the first time, 200 after). The same id with another body, or a draft id another job holds, is 409. The creator owns the field work when they hold `scope`; an office user's job has no field owner until S5b-1. |
| `GET /api/jobs`               | `jobs_read`               | `scope=company` (owner and office; their default) or `scope=mine` (the jobs the caller made or owns in the field); an estimator gets only `mine` and `scope=company` is 403. Newest first, `limit` 1 to 50, `cursor` for the next page; archived jobs are left out.                                                                                                                         |
| `GET /api/jobs/{id}`          | `jobs_read`               | The job with its customer and claim.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `PUT /api/jobs/{id}/customer` | `scope` or `coordination` | Customer name, phone, email and the property address (owner decision J3: the estimator who owns the field work, the owner and office, while the job is unsigned; the estimator's `scope`, office's `coordination`).                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `PUT /api/jobs/{id}/claim`    | `scope` or `coordination` | The claim fields the documents print; either key suffices, as in the table above.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

Every job route goes through one job-access function (`web/src/lib/jobs/access.ts`), in this order: the job is in the caller's company (otherwise 404), an estimator's own job (created it, owns its field work, or is assigned to it now, S5b-1; any other estimator's job is 404, the same answer as no such job), archived (410), then the capability (403). A route that adds to the field work (a new photo, Submit, Back to editing) needs the caller to own the job's field work, whatever their role: the company owner too only once it is theirs, by a first capture or a move to themself (403 `not_field_owner`). A customer or claim save needs an estimator to own the field work, not only be assigned the job (403 `not_field_owner`); the owner and office save them by their role. A Submit whose caller loses the job or its field work while it runs is stopped and its draft released, answered as that refusal (403 `not_field_owner` or 404). A signed job's customer and claim are refused (409 `job_signed`); after signing only office and the owner correct them (S5b-4).

The two saves carry the `revision` the phone last saw and a `changeId` it made for this change. A save whose revision is not the job's current one is 409 `stale_revision` (the answer says the current revision). The same `changeId` again with the same values is the same change: the answer is the saved job, with no second revision and no second audit row, so a lost response never turns into a conflict; the same `changeId` with other values is 409. Every write adds one audit row with the job id and revision only.

## Office jobs routes (rebuilt app, S5b-1)

`web/src/lib/jobs/office.ts`, five route files under `/api/jobs/{id}`, all `memberRoute`, every one through the job-access function above. New capability key: `field_owner_transfer` (owner only, owner decision J5). An office user starts a job with `POST /api/jobs` as anyone does; its body holds no scope, so the job starts empty, with no field owner (its draft has no `fieldOwnerUid`).

| Route                                              | Who                            | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `GET /api/jobs/{id}/coordination`                  | `coordination` or `assignment` | The office notes, the estimators assigned now, who owns the field work, and the company's active estimators to assign. An estimator is refused (403): an estimator sees only the customer view (`GET /api/jobs/{id}`), never office notes, assignments or access lists.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `PUT /api/jobs/{id}/coordination`                  | `coordination`                 | The office notes, with the coordination `revision` (the coordination view's, 0 until the first note or field owner change) and a `changeId`, as the customer save: a stale revision is 409 `stale_revision`, the same change again is the same answer. Office notes are coordination, not the signed record, so a signed job keeps them.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/assignments`                  | `assignment`                   | `{ id, estimatorActorId }`, the id made on the phone: 201 the first time, 200 for the same request again, 409 `conflict` for the same id with another body. Only an active estimator of the company may be assigned (409 `not_assignable`), once at a time (409 `already_assigned`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `DELETE /api/jobs/{id}/assignments/{assignmentId}` | `assignment`                   | Ends the assignment (the row is kept, ended); sent again, the same answer. An assignment of another job is 404 `assignment_not_found`. The estimator who owns the job's field work stays assigned until the owner moves it (409 `assigned_field_owner`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/field-owner`                  | `scope`                        | "First capture makes me field owner" (docs/spec.md 6.2): on a job nobody owns in the field yet, the caller (who may see the job: its creator, an assigned estimator, the owner) becomes its field owner, once; Postgres first, then the server sets the draft's `fieldOwnerUid` in a Firestore transaction. 201 when it was set now, 200 when the caller already owned it (it sets every draft of the job again, on a signed job too), 409 `field_owner_taken` for anyone else, 409 `job_signed` for a first capture on a signed job. A draft step that did not get through is 503 `draft_unavailable`, and the same request again sets it.                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/field-owner/transfer`         | `field_owner_transfer`         | Owner decision J5: `{ revision, changeId, toActorId }`, with the coordination revision, moves the field work to the owner themself or to an active estimator assigned to the job (409 `not_assignable` otherwise), audited with both actor ids; 409 `stale_revision` as above, and `job_signed` for a move on a signed job. The same change again is the same answer (and sets the drafts again), and so is a move to the person who owns the field work already, on a signed job too: it changes nothing and sends the field owner to every draft of the job once more. The notes and the field owner move the coordination revision, never the job revision, so neither voids a reviewed estimate or a signing link. The phone that held it is refused by the field rules from then on and keeps its unsent changes (S6 offers Save as new draft). |

Every write adds one audit row (ids and revisions only): `job_coordination_saved`, `job_estimator_assigned`, `job_estimator_unassigned`, `job_field_owner_claimed`, `job_field_owner_moved`. Becoming the field owner grants no other key: prices and approvals stay the owner's (docs/spec.md 6.2).

## Field database job locators (S6 follow-up)

`companies/{companyId}/jobs/{jobId}` is server-written, containing only the current draft ID and SQL revision. Its single-document `get` requires the same tenant, unexpired company access, second-step policy and field-draft read permission as the target draft: owner/office may read company drafts; an estimator only their field-owned draft. A locator never grants access by itself. Collection queries and every client create/update/delete are denied. No new capability or API route is introduced. Existing job create/read and Back to editing server paths project only their authorized SQL result; the SQL revision prevents older delayed writes from restoring an old draft. The phone caches the locator in the existing person-scoped field client, behind the sync gate and retention boundary. SQL customer/claim/job operations remain online-only.
