# Access matrix: Jobs routes

Part of [Access matrix](../ACCESS.md).

## Jobs routes (rebuilt app, S5a-3)

`web/src/lib/jobs/`, five handlers under `/api/jobs`, all `memberRoute`; the company is the caller's own from the sign-in and no path, query or body names one. No new capability key: each route uses keys the table above already has.

| Route                         | Who                       | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `POST /api/jobs`              | `jobs_create`             | The phone sends the job id and the first draft id. The job row is written first, then its Firestore field draft; sent again, it makes whichever of the two is missing (201 the first time, 200 after). The same id with another body, or a draft id already claimed (`job_drafts`, 0016: by another job, now or earlier), is 409; so is a draft id the field database holds for another job (security audit F1: each draft names its job in `jobId`), checked before the job row commits, so a refused id changes nothing. A retry holds the draft id for the job in `job_drafts`, committed before it touches the draft, and a draft made before `jobId` takes the job id then. The creator owns the field work when they hold `scope`; an office user's job has no field owner until S5b-1. |
| `GET /api/jobs`               | `jobs_read`               | `scope=company` (owner and office; their default) or `scope=mine` (the jobs the caller made or owns in the field); an estimator gets only `mine` and `scope=company` is 403. Newest first, `limit` 1 to 50, `cursor` for the next page; archived jobs are left out.                                                                                                                         |
| `GET /api/jobs/{id}`          | `jobs_read`               | The job with its customer and claim. For the job's field owner alone, `draftMissing: true` when the job is `draft`, the field database holds no draft under its current draft ID and a committed submission exists to rebuild it from (Start field work recovery); a lookup that does not get through leaves it out.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `PUT /api/jobs/{id}/customer` | `scope` or `coordination` | Customer name, phone, email and the property address (owner decision J3: the estimator who owns the field work, the owner and office, while the job is unsigned; the estimator's `scope`, office's `coordination`).                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `PUT /api/jobs/{id}/claim`    | `scope` or `coordination` | The claim fields the documents print; either key suffices, as in the table above.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

Every job route goes through one job-access function (`web/src/lib/jobs/access.ts`), in this order: the job is in the caller's company (otherwise 404), an estimator's own job (created it, owns its field work, or is assigned to it now, S5b-1; any other estimator's job is 404, the same answer as no such job), archived (410), then the capability (403). A route that adds to the field work (a new photo, Submit, Back to editing) needs the caller to own the job's field work, whatever their role: the company owner too only once it is theirs, by a first capture or a move to themself (403 `not_field_owner`). A customer or claim save needs an estimator to own the field work, not only be assigned the job (403 `not_field_owner`); the owner and office save them by their role. A Submit whose caller loses the job or its field work while it runs is stopped and its draft released, answered as that refusal (403 `not_field_owner` or 404). A signed job's customer and claim are refused (409 `job_signed`); after signing only office and the owner correct them (S5b-4).

The two saves carry the `revision` the phone last saw and a `changeId` it made for this change. A save whose revision is not the job's current one is 409 `stale_revision` (the answer says the current revision). The same `changeId` again with the same values is the same change: the answer is the saved job, with no second revision and no second audit row, so a lost response never turns into a conflict; the same `changeId` with other values is 409. Every write adds one audit row with the job id and revision only.

## Office jobs routes (rebuilt app, S5b-1)

`web/src/lib/jobs/office.ts`, five route files under `/api/jobs/{id}`, all `memberRoute`, every one through the job-access function above. New capability key: `field_owner_transfer` (owner only, owner decision J5). An office user starts a job with `POST /api/jobs` as anyone does; its body holds no scope, so the job starts empty, with no field owner (its draft has no `fieldOwnerUid`).

| Route                                              | Who                            | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `GET /api/jobs/{id}/coordination`                  | `coordination` or `assignment` | The office notes, the estimators assigned now, who owns the field work, and the company's active estimators to assign. An estimator is refused (403): an estimator sees only the customer view (`GET /api/jobs/{id}`), never office notes, assignments or access lists.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `PUT /api/jobs/{id}/coordination`                  | `coordination`                 | The office notes, with the coordination `revision` (the coordination view's, 0 until the first note or field owner change) and a `changeId`, as the customer save: a stale revision is 409 `stale_revision`, the same change again is the same answer. Office notes are coordination, not the signed record, so a signed job keeps them.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/assignments`                  | `assignment`                   | `{ id, estimatorActorId }`, the id made on the phone: 201 the first time, 200 for the same request again, 409 `conflict` for the same id with another body. Only an active estimator of the company may be assigned (409 `not_assignable`), once at a time (409 `already_assigned`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `DELETE /api/jobs/{id}/assignments/{assignmentId}` | `assignment`                   | Ends the assignment (the row is kept, ended); sent again, the same answer. An assignment of another job is 404 `assignment_not_found`. The estimator who owns the job's field work stays assigned until the owner moves it (409 `assigned_field_owner`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/field-owner`                  | `scope`                        | "First capture makes me field owner" (docs/spec.md 6.2): on a job nobody owns in the field yet, the caller (who may see the job: its creator, an assigned estimator, the owner) becomes its field owner, once; Postgres first, then the server sets the draft's `fieldOwnerUid` in a Firestore transaction. 201 when it was set now, 200 when the caller already owned it (it sets every draft of the job again, on a signed job too), 409 `field_owner_taken` for anyone else, 409 `job_signed` for a first capture on a signed job. A draft made for another job is never named (security audit F1): the drafts named are the ones `job_drafts` claims for the job, and a job whose current draft another job claimed is 409 `conflict`. A current draft the field database does not hold, on a job being edited (`draft`) whose current draft `job_drafts` claims, is made first from the job's latest committed submission with its provenance, as Back to editing's retry does (`web/src/lib/jobs/office.ts` `remakeCurrentDraft`): closed, naming nobody, until this step names the field owner Postgres holds just before; a job archived while it was made has it closed again. A draft that is not one Back to editing made, or a draft step that did not get through, is 503 `draft_unavailable`, and the same request again sets it.                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/field-owner/transfer`         | `field_owner_transfer`         | Owner decision J5: `{ revision, changeId, toActorId }`, with the coordination revision, moves the field work to the owner themself or to an active estimator assigned to the job (409 `not_assignable` otherwise), audited with both actor ids; 409 `stale_revision` as above, and `job_signed` for a move on a signed job. The same change again is the same answer (and sets the drafts again), and so is a move to the person who owns the field work already, on a signed job too: it changes nothing and sends the field owner to every draft of the job once more. The drafts named are the ones `job_drafts` claims for the job, as for the first capture (409 `conflict` when another job claimed its current draft), and a missing current draft is made as there. The notes and the field owner move the coordination revision, never the job revision, so neither voids a reviewed estimate or a signing link. The phone that held it is refused by the field rules from then on and keeps its unsent changes (S6 offers Save as new draft). |

Every write adds one audit row (ids and revisions only): `job_coordination_saved`, `job_estimator_assigned`, `job_estimator_unassigned`, `job_field_owner_claimed`, `job_field_owner_moved`. Becoming the field owner grants no other key: prices and approvals stay the owner's (docs/spec.md 6.2).

## Field database job locators (S6 follow-up)

`companies/{companyId}/jobs/{jobId}` is server-written, containing only the current draft ID and SQL revision. Its single-document `get` requires the same tenant, unexpired company access, second-step policy and field-draft read permission as the target draft: owner/office may read company drafts; an estimator only their field-owned draft. A locator never grants access by itself. Collection queries and every client create/update/delete are denied. No new capability or API route is introduced. Existing job create/read and Back to editing server paths project only their authorized SQL result; the SQL revision prevents older delayed writes from restoring an old draft. The phone caches the locator in the existing person-scoped field client, behind the sync gate and retention boundary. SQL customer/claim/job operations remain online-only.

## Archive, restore and copy (rebuilt app, S5b-2)

`web/src/lib/jobs/archive.ts` and `archive-store.ts`, three route files under `/api/jobs/{id}`, all `memberRoute`, and the owner's archived list. No new capability key: `jobs_archive` (owner) and `jobs_copy` (every role) are in [the table](roles.md). No migration: `jobs.archived_at` and `archived_by_actor_id` are 0010's.

| Route                          | Who            | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------ | -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/archive`  | `jobs_archive` | `{ revision, changeId }`, as the customer save. Files away an unsigned draft (a draft or a submitted job): the job leaves every list but the archived list, every other job route answers 410 `archived`, and its current field draft becomes `archived`, which the field rules refuse every client write to. Nothing is deleted. A signed job has no archive (409 `job_signed`). While a Submit of the job is still deciding or writing its field marker, 409 `submission_in_progress` (try again shortly).     |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/restore`  | `jobs_archive` | `{ revision, changeId }`. Puts an archived job back as it was: `submitted` when its current draft has a committed submission, otherwise `draft`, and its field draft back to `submitted` or `editing`. A job that is not archived is 409 `not_archived`. A signing link the archive voided stays voided (the job revision moved on).                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/copy`     | `jobs_copy`    | `{ id, draftId }`, both made on the phone: a new draft job from a signed job (409 `not_signed` otherwise), made by the caller as a new job (who holds `scope` owns its field work; office's copy has none). It holds the signed record's property address, field map and rooms, less the scope confirmation (`scopeConfirmed`); not the customer, the claim or the photos, which stay with the signed job. 201 the first time, 200 for the same request again, 409 `conflict` for the same id with another body, a draft id already claimed (`job_drafts`), or one the field database holds for another job. |
| `GET /api/jobs?scope=archived` | `jobs_archive` | The company's archived jobs, newest first, paged as the other lists.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

Archive and restore reach an archived job through the job-access function with one option no other route uses. Each step is Postgres first, then the field draft: a field step that does not get through answers 503 `draft_unavailable`, and the same request again (the same `changeId`, or the same copy ids) answers the same and makes it. The draft keeps `archiveRevision`, the job revision of its last archive or restore, so a slower request never undoes a newer one. Every write adds one audit row (ids and revisions only): `job_archived`, `job_restored`, `job_copied` (on the new job, naming the job it copied).
