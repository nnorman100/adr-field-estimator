# Access matrix: Proposals and approvals

Part of [Access matrix](../ACCESS.md).

## Proposals and approvals (rebuilt app, S5b-8)

`web/src/lib/approvals/`, four handlers on three paths under `/api/jobs/{id}/approvals`, each `requireFlag('priced_extras', ...)` (a company with priced extras off gets 404 on every one, owner decision D5), each also 404 while `price_books` is off (a proposal reads and pins the company's published prices, as every price route requires price books), and each through the job-access function above, which answers company, own or assigned job, archived 410 and then the capability's 403 in that order; a write also rechecks, under a lock on the caller's membership row, that the caller is still an active member with the role their sign-in gave (403 `not_allowed` otherwise). The estimator (or the owner) proposes with `scope`; only the owner approves, with the new key `additions_approve`. The company is the caller's own from the sign-in; no path or body names one. Table: `job_price_approvals` (migration 0019, `docs/data/postgres-submissions.md`).

What can be proposed: a named adjustment from the job's pinned prices (S5b-7) or an hourly addition. A custom multiplier, a unit or a flat addition is refused with 400 `not_yet_supported` until the estimate can price it (firstmate ruling A1, 2026-11-20: every control an owner can set must change a price).

Every write runs in one transaction under the job's row lock (the approval also under the company row lock and the owner's active membership lock, as Reprice takes them). It is bound to the committed record of the job's current draft, the job revision the caller names and the job's current pin; none of them moves the job revision. A write is refused with 409 `not_submitted` (job still being edited), `job_signed`, `stale_revision` (the job moved since the caller's copy), `estimate_made` (the record's estimate is made: Back to editing and Finish make a new record, D1) or `repriced` (the owner repriced the record's draft: Back to editing and Finish price it again).

**The estimate (S5a-9) with approvals.** While `priced_extras` is on, `POST /api/jobs/{id}/estimate` prices the record with its rows that are approved, still open, at the job's current revision and on the pin it prices from: the approved named adjustment, and each approved hourly row as additional work at its saved rate, printed "Additional work, not in {company} price sheet: {description}, {hours} h at ${rate}/h, approved by {name} on {date}." An open hourly row not yet approved goes to the estimate as additional work with no approval: it adds no money, and the estimate cannot be signed until the owner settles it. The job revision the estimate was priced at is stored with it, so the documents rebuilt after a later customer save, and the pinned side of the owner's reprice preview, price the same rows and the same total. With the switch off the estimate ignores approvals. Another company's job is 404 to every role on every route above, and nothing is written.
