# Access matrix: Proposals and approvals

Part of [Access matrix](../ACCESS.md).

## Proposals and approvals (rebuilt app, S5b-8)

`web/src/lib/approvals/`, four handlers on three paths under `/api/jobs/{id}/approvals`, each `requireFlag('priced_extras', ...)` (a company with priced extras off gets 404 on every one, owner decision D5) and each through the job-access function above. The estimator (or the owner) proposes with `scope`; only the owner approves, with the new key `additions_approve`. The company is the caller's own from the sign-in; no path or body names one. Table: `job_price_approvals` (migration 0019, `docs/data/postgres-submissions.md`).

What can be proposed: a named adjustment from the job's pinned prices (S5b-7) or an hourly addition. A custom multiplier, a unit or a flat addition is refused with 400 `not_yet_supported` until the estimate can price it (firstmate ruling A1, 2026-11-20: every control an owner can set must change a price).

Every write runs in one transaction under the job's row lock (the approval also under the company row lock and the owner's active membership lock, as Reprice takes them). It is bound to the committed record of the job's current draft, the job revision the caller names and the job's current pin; none of them moves the job revision. A write is refused with 409 `not_submitted` (job still being edited), `job_signed`, `stale_revision` (the job moved since the caller's copy), `estimate_made` (the record's estimate is made: Back to editing and Finish make a new record, D1) or `repriced` (the owner repriced the record's draft: Back to editing and Finish price it again).

| Route | Who | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /api/jobs/{id}/approvals` | `jobs_read` (any job reader) | The submitted record's open proposals and approvals at the job's revision, oldest first: `{ job: { revision }, approvals: [{ id, kind, adjustmentId, description, hours, rate, jobRevision, proposedAt, approved, approvedAt, approverName }] }`. An empty list for a job being edited. Writes nothing. |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/approvals` | `scope` (owner, estimator) | Proposes. Body (Zod, strict): `{ id, revision, kind: 'named_adjustment', adjustmentId }` or `{ id, revision, kind: 'hourly', description, hours }`; `id` made on the phone; `description` one line of 1 to 300 characters kept exactly as entered, holding a character that is not whitespace (Unicode spaces included) and no control or line-separator character; `hours` 0.25 to 200 with at most two decimal places. The first proposal on a job with no pin pins it to the company's newest published version (sequence 1, `first_price`, with its `job_price_pinned` audit row), as the first estimate would; the estimate then prices from that pin and makes no second one. An hourly row takes the pinned table's additional work rate (its one enabled `extras` item), saved on the row (D3): 409 `no_hourly_rate` when there is none or it is above 10,000. A named adjustment the pinned prices do not have is 409 `unknown_adjustment`; none is seeded. 201 the first time; the same id and body again 200 with nothing written; the same id with another body, or for another job, 409 `conflict`. One audit row `price_proposed` (the row id and kind). |
| `PUT /api/jobs/{id}/approvals/{approvalId}` | `scope` (owner, estimator) | Edits a proposal: the body is a new proposal of the same kind with a new `id`. In one transaction the row named ends (`edited`, its approval with it) and the new one is proposed, unapproved, so any edit clears the approval. 404 `approval_not_found`; 409 `approval_closed` when the row named has already ended; 409 `conflict` for another kind; 409 `stale_revision` for a row proposed at an older job revision (propose anew). The same new id and body again 200. One audit row `price_edited` (the old and new ids) beside the new row's `price_proposed`. |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/approvals/{approvalId}/approve` | `additions_approve` (owner only) | Approves the proposal exactly as proposed. Body `{ revision, approverName? }`: an hourly approval needs `approverName`, the name as the owner enters it (1 to 150 characters, kept exactly as entered; it prints on the proposal as today's Additional work does), 400 `approver_name_required`; any other kind refuses one (400 `invalid_request`). A named adjustment approved while another is approved on the record ends that one (`replaced`, D4). The same approval again by the same owner with the same name is 200 as the first time; an ended row, or one another approval already settled, is 409 `approval_closed`. One audit row `price_approved` (the row id and kind). Estimator and office are 403. |

**The estimate (S5a-9) with approvals.** While `priced_extras` is on, `POST /api/jobs/{id}/estimate` prices the record with its rows that are approved, still open, at the job's current revision and on the pin it prices from: the approved named adjustment, and each approved hourly row as additional work at its saved rate, printed "Additional work, not in {company} price sheet: {description}, {hours} h at ${rate}/h, approved by {name} on {date}." With the switch off the estimate ignores approvals. Another company's job is 404 to every role on every route above, and nothing is written.
