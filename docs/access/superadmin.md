# Access matrix: Superadmin

Part of [Access matrix](../ACCESS.md).

## Superadmin

Not a company role and not a membership. Can create/suspend/reactivate companies and invite or replace owners (or create an owner login with a temporary password). Read-only, audited support access to any company's profile, jobs, documents, photos, evidence, team, invitations, price books, job summaries, AI usage and drying watch, plus listed support actions: resend or cancel a staff invitation, switch a non-owner between estimator and office, re-enable a disabled member, archive or restore an unsigned draft (also in a suspended company). Never edits customer jobs.

In the rebuilt app (`web/`, S3-9) this is the platform operator: an actor with no company in the operator-only sign-in tenant, added only by the owner-run `npm run platform:add-operator` (no API adds one), signing in with an authenticator app every time and again after 12 hours. Changing a company's status, binding its sign-in tenant, switching a feature flag, setting AI ceilings, and every team or second-step support action also need a sign-in under 15 minutes old (D7; status, tenant, flags and AI ceilings added 2026-11-14, D-030), or the route answers 401 `sign_in_again` and changes nothing; listing companies, creating one and the read-only views keep the 12-hour sign-in. The screens are at `/operator/{operator tenant id}` (S3-15) and call only those routes. Every route under `/api/platform/` is an `operatorRoute`; an owner, estimator or office user gets 403 `not_an_operator` there. The operator lists and creates companies, suspends, reactivates and archives them (archive needs suspended), binds a company to its sign-in tenant once, invites or replaces the owner by link (replacing disables the old owner and revokes their sessions), resets a member's second step after a phone check (logged; no email until S9, so the operator tells them by phone), turns one of the known feature flags on or off for a company (the only way a flag changes; no company role can, an owner included), sets a company's daily and monthly AI ceilings (S7-4; no company role can), reads a company's team and its prices (current version, working copy and version list) read-only, and takes the support actions above on the team (resend or cancel an invitation, switch estimator and office, re-enable a member; a company with no active owner keeps its pending owner invitation). Each action writes one audit row naming the operator; the team view and the prices view write one per view; a refused action writes none. There is no temporary-password owner login (D11).
