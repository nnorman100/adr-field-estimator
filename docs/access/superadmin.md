# Access matrix: Superadmin

Part of [Access matrix](../ACCESS.md).

## Superadmin

Not a company role and not a membership. Can create/suspend/reactivate companies and invite or replace owners (or create an owner login with a temporary password). Read-only, audited support access to any company's profile, jobs, documents, photos, evidence, team, invitations, price books, job summaries, AI usage and drying watch, plus listed support actions: resend or cancel a staff invitation, switch a non-owner between estimator and office, re-enable a disabled member, archive or restore an unsigned draft (also in a suspended company; historical: dropped by owner decision 2026-11-19, S5b-5, see below). Never edits customer jobs.

In the rebuilt app (`web/`, S3-9) this is the platform operator: an actor with no company in the operator-only sign-in tenant, added only by the owner-run `npm run platform:add-operator` (no API adds one), signing in with an authenticator app every time and again after 12 hours. Changing a company's status, binding its sign-in tenant, switching a feature flag, setting AI ceilings, every team or second-step support action and the records check also need a sign-in under 15 minutes old (D7; status, tenant, flags and AI ceilings added 2026-11-14, D-030; the records check by its step, S8-3), or the route answers 401 `sign_in_again` and changes nothing; listing companies, creating one and the read-only team and prices views keep the 12-hour sign-in. The screens are at `/operator/{operator tenant id}` (S3-15) and call only those routes. Every route under `/api/platform/` is an `operatorRoute`; an owner, estimator or office user gets 403 `not_an_operator` there. The operator lists and creates companies, suspends, reactivates and archives them (archive needs suspended), binds a company to its sign-in tenant once, invites or replaces the owner by link (replacing disables the old owner and revokes their sessions), resets a member's second step after a phone check (logged; no email until S9, so the operator tells them by phone), turns one of the known feature flags on or off for a company (the only way a flag changes; no company role can, an owner included), sets a company's daily and monthly AI ceilings (S7-4; no company role can), reads a company's team and its prices (current version, working copy and version list) read-only, reads a company's jobs read-only (below; S5b-5), runs the records check on a company after a restore (S8-3, below), and takes the support actions above on the team (resend or cancel an invitation, switch estimator and office, re-enable a member; a company with no active owner keeps its pending owner invitation). Each action writes one audit row naming the operator; the team view, the prices view and each jobs view write one per view, the records check one per run; a refused action writes none. There is no temporary-password owner login (D11). The operator does not archive or restore a job (owner decision 2026-11-19, S5b-5): every write policy on the job tables refuses an operator, so the operator asks the company owner, who holds `jobs_archive`, to.

## Operator-only actions (rebuilt app)

Actions only the platform operator takes. No company role holds them, an owner included, so none is a capability key in [Company roles](roles.md). `OPERATOR_ACTIONS` in `web/src/lib/access/roles.ts` lists the same names, and `web/tests/unit/operator-actions.test.ts` reads this table and fails when the two differ.
