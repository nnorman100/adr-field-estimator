# Access matrix: Company profile API

Part of [Access matrix](../ACCESS.md).

## Company profile API (rebuilt app, S5a-4 and S5a-4b)

The five lines the four documents print: address, phone, email, licence and sign-off. They live on the company's own row (migration 0010, no new migration). The company name is read-only here: the platform operator sets it when the company is made. The logo is not stored yet (decision J9: text letterhead until then). Both routes are `memberRoute`s and check, in this order: the company in the path is the caller's own (else 404, as for any other company's id), the caller holds `profile_manage` (else 403, before the body is read), and only then the body (Zod, strict: a body that names any other field, including the name or status, is a 400). An estimator or office user is refused on the read as well as the write, so nothing of the profile reaches their phone; the Company settings screen does not request it for them.

| Route                             | Key              | Writes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `GET /api/companies/{id}/profile` | `profile_manage` | nothing; the answer also holds the profile's `revision`, the letterhead lines and document settings (S5a-4b), and lists what the engine's setup check says is missing (company name, address, CSLB licence, document sign-off, and before signing the start-work authorization and payment terms)                                                                                    |
| `PUT /api/companies/{id}/profile` | `profile_manage` | the profile columns of the company's own row (0010's five, and 0013's letterhead lines and settings), only while the `revision` the save names is still the stored one; audit `company_profile_saved` holding the actor, the change id and the number of fields changed (each letterhead line and each document setting counts as one), never a value, and none when nothing changed |

An empty line means not set. The limits are those of migration 0010's check constraints (address 500, phone 80, email 254, licence 80, sign-off 150 characters; no control characters; the email is stored in lower case). A platform operator cannot change a company's profile (the 0010 trigger), and no support action does.

Since S5a-4b (migration 0013) the same routes read and save the letterhead lines (`emergencyPhone`, `fax`, `website`, `tagline`, `mailingAddress`: one line each, at most 80, 80, 200, 150 and 500 characters) and the document settings (`documents`: `proposal_layout` `itemized` or `letter`; `company_clauses`, up to 10, each a title of up to 120 characters and a text of up to 2,000; `prep_tasks`, up to 30 of up to 300 characters; `photo_log_grouping` `room` or `shot_tag`; `authorization_text` and `payment_terms_text`, up to 4,000 characters each, line breaks kept; `show_previous_price`; at most 64,000 bytes as JSON). The settings are one Zod schema (`web/src/lib/profile/settings.ts`), checked when saved and again when read, so a stored value that does not read is never used (the read answers 503). A save may leave the letterhead lines and the settings out, and they keep what is stored, so a phone with an older Company settings screen cannot clear them; `documents`, when sent, is the whole settings object, and a setting left out of it is its neutral default. An unknown key, a clause or list over its limit, or a layout or grouping outside its list is a 400 that writes nothing. Every company starts with the letterhead empty and the settings neutral (itemized, no clauses or prep tasks, by room, no texts, no previous price); no company's values are seeded. Line prices on the proposal stay the price table's `show_line_breakdown`. The letterhead lines and the settings are printed by later steps (S5a-9b, S5a-9c, S5a-10, S5a-11, S5b-6): since S5a-9b the proposal prints the letterhead lines that are set, the company's layout and its clauses; the documents' reader still returns the photo log grouping as by room until S5a-9c.

Every save carries the `revision` it was based on (the company row's `updated_at`, as the read returns it; no new column) and a `changeId` made on the phone (a uuid). A save that asks for exactly what is stored answers 200 with the current revision, so a retry after a lost answer is not a conflict. Otherwise, a save whose revision is no longer the stored one writes nothing and answers 409 `stale_revision` with the current profile, and the Company settings screen shows that profile and tells the owner it was changed somewhere else.
