# Access matrix: Company isolation gate

Part of [Access matrix](../ACCESS.md).

## Company isolation gate

The standard's required test, one named suite (S3-17), run by `npm run test:isolation` in `web/` and by the `company-isolation` job of `.github/workflows/web.yml`, which `Web app / check` requires. It seeds two companies in two sign-in tenants, then as each company's owner, estimator and office user calls every route with the other company's ids (the photo route's bytes and originals among them):

- **Routes** (`web/tests/db/company-isolation.test.ts`): no sign-in answers 401; the other company's people are refused (401, 403 or 404) on every route in the matrix but four open answers the suite lists (`/api/me`, the sign-in page, health and echo) with no id, email, address or tenant of the company in the body, and the database, the sign-in tenant calls and the access projection are unchanged. Also the caller's own company with the other's record ids (an invitation, member or job of the other company), the caller's own team and job lists (no row of the other company), a job create that carries the other company's job and draft ids (it makes only the caller's own job and draft, and the other company's rows are unchanged), `/api/me` with the other company named in the query or a header, the scheduler's route (no person's token passes), and an invitation addressed to an email that exists in both companies. Each open answer is held to what its comment says is public: `/api/me` names the caller's own company, `GET /api/sign-in/{address}` answers a company's name and sign-in tenant and nothing else, and a new open route fails until its answer is listed.
- **The route list** (`web/tests/db/isolation-matrix.ts`): the suite finds and imports every `route.ts` under `web/src/app` and fails when an exported handler has no row in the matrix or a row names a handler that no longer exists; every exported handler but the create handlers is then called as above, and the suite fails by name on a handler it did not reach or a path parameter it has no id for (a create handler is called by its own test above). Each row's kind (company, invitation, operator, service, session, public, link) records the decision for review; a link route (S5a-11b) reads no sign-in, so every company user's call carries no link token and is refused. A route that names no company (the Prices routes: the company is the sign-in's) is marked `self`: the other company's user is answered about their own company, so the suite holds that answer to no trace of the other company (the seeded working copies and versions carry marker names) and no change, and the route's write carries a stale revision so it is refused. Adding a route means adding its row, which a reviewer reads.
- **The S5a records** (`web/tests/db/company-isolation-records.test.ts`, S5a-12, in the same `npm run test:isolation`): both companies first get everything S5a makes, through the real routes: a job submitted, priced and signed by the homeowner through a link (its submission, price pin, estimate, agreement, signature image and the two document links made at signing) and a second job with a pending signing link. The S5a routes are read from the generated route list (`web/src/lib/api-routes.generated.ts`, everything under `/api/jobs`, `/api/companies/{id}/profile`, `/api/sign` and `/api/documents`), which must agree with the route files the matrix reads, so a new S5a route is called here without an edit and fails until it has a matrix row. Then: every signed-in S5a route as the other company's owner, estimator and office user, on both of the company's jobs with its real link, photo and signature ids, is refused (401, 403 or 404) with no id, hash, name or token of the company in the answer and no change in the database; the caller's own job with the other company's link, photo or signature id is refused; each company's own reads and lists hold its own records and none of the other's; and every link route called with each of the other company's own live tokens (a pending signing link, a used one, the homeowner's and the adjuster's document links) and this company's ids answers nothing of it, refuses its photos and a signature it did not upload, and leaves its rows as they were.
- **Every company table** (same file): each table of the `public` schema with a `company_id` column, found from the catalog, must hold rows of both companies after that flow (a table the flow does not fill is named in the suite with where its own check is, so a new company table fails until it is filled or named). As the runtime role in one company's context, none of the other company's rows shows and the company's own do; inserting a copy of one of the other company's rows (made to look new, so the table's own insert guard lets it reach the row policy), changing it, moving it into the caller's company, moving the caller's own rows into it and deleting it are all refused by the policy or the grant (42501) or reach no row.
- **SQL as the runtime role** (`web/tests/db/company-isolation.test.ts`): every table in the `public` schema, found from the catalog, read inside each company's context shows no trace of the other company, with no company set it shows nothing, and inserts, updates and deletes aimed at the other company's rows are refused or change no row.
- **The operator**: a support view of a company's team writes one `support_team_viewed` audit row naming the operator and that company; the other company never sees it.
- **Firestore rules** (`web/tests/emu/rules/company-isolation.test.ts`, emulator): every user of one company is refused every read, list, write, delete and mixed batch on the other's company, access records, drafts, rooms and photos; so are a sign-in with no tenant, no sign-in, a same-uid account of another tenant and an access record written for the wrong company's person. Since S5a-12 also the records S5a leaves there, in both companies: a draft fenced by Submit (`submitting`), a `submitted` one and the next draft after Back to editing, each with field map v2 rooms (items, prep tasks) and photo references with a capture time. The other company's people are refused every read and list of them, the "my drafts" query on the company's drafts (with their own uid and with the company's field owner's), and the writes the draft's own field owner makes (the field map, a new room, a changed photo, a removed room, all in one batch); the field owner then makes the same writes, so each refusal is the company boundary and not a malformed write.
- **Real services on `test`** (owner, S5a-12): the create-only upload on the evidence bucket, the fence on `dryscope-field-test`, a double submit against Cloud Run and a homeowner signing a link on a real iPhone, in [S5a real-service checks](../runbook/s5a-real-service-checks.md), with their results recorded there and never mixed with the CI results above. They are pending and not yet run; S5a is not closed until all four are recorded.
- **Smoke check for `test`** (`web/scripts/smoke-isolation.ts`, read-only, fake data only): with the sign-ins of two test companies, one company's token and the other's ids are refused on the team, operator and invitation routes, and each token's `/api/me` names its own company. It sends no token to a service whose health does not say `test`. The owner runs it with real test sign-ins in S3-18; no workflow has those sign-ins, so none runs it.
