# Access matrix: AI usage and ceilings

Part of [Access matrix](../ACCESS.md).

## AI usage and ceilings (rebuilt app, S7-4)

`web/src/lib/ai/` (`budget.ts` the ledger, `settings.ts` the ceiling change, `http.ts` the two handlers). Each company has a daily and a monthly AI ceiling, both counting all AI per UTC day and UTC month; with nothing stored they are $10 a day and $200 a month (owner decision V5 B, `web/src/lib/ai/ceilings.ts`), and the operator's stored ceilings replace them. Every AI call reserves before work and is refused with 429 and `Retry-After` (field work continues) when the reservation, the person's daily quota for that feature, or either ceiling does not allow it. Neither route takes or returns a price of the company's work.

| Route                                                 | Who                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Writes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `GET /api/companies/{id}/ai-usage`                    | `ai_usage_read` (the owner). Checked in this order: the company in the path is the caller's own (else 404, as for any other company's id), then the key (else 403, an estimator or office user). The Company settings screen asks for it only for a role holding the key | nothing; the answer holds, for today and this month, the ceiling, whether it is stored or the default, what is used (settled costs plus reservations not settled yet), the part not settled yet and when the period starts again                                                                                                                                              |
| `PUT /api/platform/companies/{companyId}/ai-settings` | platform operator (`operatorRoute`; an owner, estimator or office user gets 403 `not_an_operator`)                                                                                                                                                                       | the company's two ceilings (`company_ai_settings`, whole micro-USD or null for the default; daily not above monthly), stamped with the operator, and one audit row `ai_ceilings_set` with the new and previous ceilings; an unknown company is 404 and a bad body 400, and either writes nothing. The company's voice choice is never changed here (migration 0012's trigger) |

The six AI feature switches (`live_voice`, `ai_summary`, `ai_scope_fill`, `ai_meter_reading`, `ai_voice_notes`, `ai_job_summary`) are company flags in `FLAG_NAMES` (`web/src/lib/flags/known.ts`), each with an owner, a purpose and a removal date, all off by default; the operator turns one on with the existing flag route. They add no capability key: the AI boundary refuses a feature whose switch is not on.

## AI summary draft (rebuilt app, S7-6)

`POST /api/jobs/{id}/ai/summary-draft` (`web/src/lib/summary-draft/`) asks for an AI draft of the job's findings summary. It adds no capability key. Checked in this order: the job is in the caller's own company and seen by them (else 404, as for any other company's job), not archived (410), the caller holds `audit` (the owner and an estimator; else 403) and owns the job's field work (else 403 `not_field_owner`, since only the field owner can write the draft), with no Submit of its current draft pending or committed (else 409 `draft_not_editing`); then the company's `ai_summary` switch (off answers 403 `ai_feature_off`, and nothing is read or spent); then the phone's call id (the same id again answers the stored proposal; an id used for another job, draft, person or call is 409 `call_reused`). It writes one `ai_ledger` row (the spend) and one `ai_results` row (the proposal, `proposed`), and never the field draft.
