# Access matrix: Signing on the phone

Part of [Access matrix](../ACCESS.md).

## Signing on the phone (rebuilt app, S5a-11)

`web/src/lib/agreements/`, two handlers on one path, `/api/jobs/{id}/agreements`, both `requireFlag('price_books', ...)` (a company without price books gets 404) and both through the job-access function above. No new capability key: signing is `sign` (owner, estimator), and there is no owner approval before signing (J2).

| Route                            | Who                                                  | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/agreements` | `sign` (owner, estimator)                            | Body `{ id, estimateId, proposalSha256, signatureId, signerName, documentsConfirmed: true }`, every id made on the phone; the name is trimmed, 1 to 150 characters, no control character. One transaction under the job's row lock: the same id and body is the same agreement (200), the same id with another body or for another job 409 `conflict`; then 409 `already_signed` (one original agreement per job), `not_submitted`, `company_setup_missing` with the setup check's own `missing` list (the four company fields, the start-work authorization and the payment terms), `estimate_superseded` when the estimate named is not the committed record of the job's current draft priced at its current pin (a new record or pin voids an older estimate for signing), `proposal_changed`, `needs_review` (a customer, claim or address save after pricing), `not_signable` (the stored documents were not signable, or the proposal does not hold both texts), `signature_missing` (no signature of this job under that id, or one received before this estimate was made: a revised estimate, after a reprice, needs a new signature). Then the agreement is inserted (signer kind `device`, the caller as the technician who presented it) with the signed record's bytes, the job becomes `signed`, and one audit row `agreement_signed` (pin and version sequence numbers). The same transaction makes the agreement's two document links (S5a-11b, "Signing links and document links" below) and the answer that signed carries their tokens once (`documentLinks`; the same request again carries none and replaces none) and the job's document links as they now are (`links`, as `GET /api/jobs/{id}/document-links` reads them). 201 the first time. Migration 0011's trigger is the backstop for the estimate, its pin and the signature object. |
| `GET /api/jobs/{id}/agreements`  | `jobs_read` (everyone with the job, office included) | The job's signed record read from the agreement's stored bytes (never rebuilt, never priced again), or `agreement: null`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

The signed record (`dryscope-signed-v1`, canonical JSON in `agreements.documents_bytes`) states who signed (the name), when (the database clock at signing, the row's own `signed_at`) and how (on the technician's phone with who presented it; a signing link, S5a-11b, names its link), and holds the signature's id and SHA-256, the proposal exactly as stored with the estimate (its canonical text hashes to `proposal_sha256`), the itemized claim, letter, photo log and claim. Since S5a-11 the proposal (format `dryscope-proposal-v2`) holds the company's start-work authorization and payment terms exactly as entered, and the estimate's setup check lists either one that is empty, so documents without them are not signable. After signing every field write (the draft stays `submitted`, so the field rules refuse it), photo upload, new signature, submission, Back to editing, estimate and customer or claim save is refused, and the stored bytes never change; a later price publish, profile change or engine release changes nothing signed. The signed page prints the stored proposal, with the authorization and payment terms, above the signature image, signer name, time and how it was signed. A job another estimator owns, or another company's, is 404, an archived job 410, office signing 403.
