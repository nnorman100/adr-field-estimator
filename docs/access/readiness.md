# Access matrix: Ready to review?

Part of [Access matrix](../ACCESS.md).

## Ready to review? (rebuilt app, S5b-11)
`web/src/lib/readiness/`, one read-only handler behind the company's `readiness` switch (`requireFlag`, off by default), through the job-access function. No new capability key: the check is scope work, as Submit is (`scope`), so the owner and the estimator with the job read it and office does not. No body and no query (a query is 400 `invalid_request`); the company is the caller's own from the sign-in.

| Route | Who | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /api/jobs/{id}/readiness` | `scope` (owner; the estimator who made the job, owns its field work or is assigned it) | The advisory gaps of the job's current editing draft: `{status: 'checked', jobId, draftId, jobRevision, fieldSha256, ready, gaps: [{code, roomId?, count?}], unavailable}`, or `{status: 'unavailable', jobId, reason}` (`not_editing` for a submitted or signed job, a draft with a Submit in progress or a fenced draft, `draft_changed` when the job changed while the draft was read, `draft_unreadable`, `field_unavailable`). Gaps name field keys, claim keys and stable room ids only, never a value, name or note. `ready` is true only with no gap and no unavailable check other than `ocr` (the meter reading, never stored yet); the jobs-list marker shows a gap count when there are gaps, "Ready to review" only for a `ready` answer, and no marker for an answer with no gap that is not `ready` (a check it could not run is not a pass). Reads Postgres (the job, its address and claim, the price version's area basis and stored photo ids) and the field draft; the field reader is built only after Postgres has allowed the read. After the field read, whatever its outcome, Postgres is read again: the caller's membership, role and the company's switch, the job's access and state (a Submit that has started since is `not_editing`), and the job's price version are checked as they are then, by the same function as the first read, in this order: company active, switch, membership, role holding `scope`, then the job (a lost job, role or switch is refused as above; a changed job or price version is `draft_changed`). A scope confirmation made before an owner reprice counts as not confirmed, as the estimate treats it. A price version this server's engine cannot price (another engine version) reads as `unavailable`, as the estimate refuses it. With no published price version the price-dependent room area check is `unavailable` and the `prices` gap is raised. Writes nothing, no audit event. `Cache-Control: no-store`. |

Switch off: 404 `not_found` before anything is read. Office: 403 `not_allowed`, checked before the job is looked up, so office gets 403 even for an archived or missing job, unlike the other job routes (which answer 410 first). A job of another company, or another estimator's job they are not assigned: 404 `not_found`. An archived job: 410 `archived`. A database failure: 503 `readiness_unavailable`. Finish, Submit, the estimate and signing never call this route, and none of its answers holds them up. `GET /api/me` tells the screens whether the switch is on (`features.readiness`); the route checks it again.
