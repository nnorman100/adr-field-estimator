# Access matrix: Submit and Back to editing

Part of [Access matrix](../ACCESS.md).

## Submit and Back to editing (rebuilt app, S5a-8)

`web/src/lib/submissions/`, two handlers, both `memberRoute` and both through the job-access function above. No new capability key: both are scope work (`scope`: owner and estimator). The company is the caller's own from the sign-in; no path or body names one.

| Route                             | Who                        | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/submissions` | `scope` (owner, estimator) | "Finish and review". The phone sends a submission id it made, the job's current draft id and the SHA-256 of the canonical form of what it showed (`web/src/lib/field/canonical.ts`). Under the job's row lock the server writes a `pending` intent row (with a server-made epoch and a two-minute expiry), then, while the caller still owns the field work and the row is still `pending`, syncs the job's drafts from Postgres (`web/src/lib/jobs/sync-job.ts`), which fences the draft (`submitting` with that row's epoch; a sync that read Postgres before a later change, such as the worker's abort, writes nothing, except in the accepted residual window of milliseconds between the sync's version check and its Firestore commit, which its catch-up closes at once: see the [test release runbook](../runbook/test-release.md)), reads it once, recomputes the digest and checks every photo reference against the evidence registry, then one conditional update decides: `committed` with the snapshot bytes (the job becomes `submitted`), or `aborted`. Only then the job's drafts are synced again: `submitted`, or back to `editing`. A draft the field database holds as submitted, or fenced by its recorded Submit, is never opened or fenced again (409 `submission_in_progress`). 201 and the record the first time, 200 and the same record for the same id and body again; the same id with another body is 409 `conflict`, an aborted id is 409 `submission_aborted` (start again with a new id). 409 `submission_in_progress` while another attempt on the draft is undecided (two phones: one record), `already_submitted` once recorded, `not_current_draft`, `digest_mismatch`, `photo_missing`, `draft_invalid`, `too_many_photos`, `draft_missing`; 503 `submissions_unavailable` when the field database cannot be reached (the attempt stays pending and the same request continues it). |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/drafts`      | `scope` (owner, estimator) | "Back to editing" (owner decision J4). The phone sends a new draft id and the submitted draft it comes from, which must be the job's current draft. Under the job's row lock the job moves to the new draft (`draft` again, one audit row), then the server makes the new Firestore draft from the submitted record (never from the old draft) with its provenance. 201 the first time, 200 for the same new id again (it makes the draft if it is still missing); another new id after that is 409 `conflict`, and so is a draft id already claimed (`job_drafts`, 0016: by another job, or an earlier draft of this one) or one the field database holds for another job or from another submission (security audit F1); when the owner repriced the job while that record's draft was its current one (S5b-6), the new draft starts with the scope confirmation cleared, so Finish needs it confirmed again; a job that is not submitted 409 `not_submitted`, a signed job 409 `job_signed`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

A job another estimator owns, or another company's, is 404 on both, an archived job is 410, and office is 403. Every write adds one audit row with the job id and counts only (`submission_started`, `submission_committed` with the revision and the room and photo counts, `submission_aborted` with a fixed reason code, `job_back_to_editing`). The scheduled worker (`POST /api/internal/access-projection`, the scheduler account only) also aborts attempts left pending past their expiry and syncs their jobs' drafts, syncs the job of a decided attempt whose request could not, writes the release again for a recently aborted row (only in companies it visits for other work; a fence a previous version, still serving during a rollout, closed late), and syncs every job whose drafts fell behind Postgres more than 60 seconds ago (`web/src/lib/jobs/sync-repair.ts`); its audit rows have no person. While a draft is `submitting` or `submitted`, the field rules refuse every phone write and the photo route refuses a new photo.

F5 draft safety bounds: room and photo document ids, and every `roomOrder` entry, must match `[A-Za-z0-9_-]{1,64}`; the phone's UUIDs still pass. `roomOrder` has at most 30 distinct string ids. Rules cannot cap collection counts without a counter, so Submit reads at most 31 rooms and 301 photo references in its fenced transaction. More than 30 rooms aborts with 409 `draft_invalid`, more than `MAX_PHOTO_REFS` (300, in `web/src/lib/field/schema.ts`) photo references with 409 `too_many_photos` (reachable only when phones race past the photo route's `photo_limit`); either releases the draft for editing; no partial snapshot reaches an estimate. The photo ceiling is an abuse-protection safety cap the owner can raise, not a target for normal field work. Well-formed but unuploaded photo ids still fail the evidence-registry check with `photo_missing`. Existing submitted and signed records stay as stored; these checks apply when submitting an editable draft. No new route, permission or index.
