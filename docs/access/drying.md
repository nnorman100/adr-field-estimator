# Access matrix: Drying log

Part of [Access matrix](../ACCESS.md).

## Drying log (rebuilt app, S5b-10)

`web/src/lib/drying/`, four handlers, `memberRoute`, through the job-access function (`jobAccess`). No new capability key and no feature switch: reading uses `jobs_read`, adding and voiding use `scope`. The company is the caller's own from the sign-in; the path names the job (and the reading) and nothing else. The readings sit beside the job, never in its field draft or signed record: adding or voiding one changes no job revision, field revision, estimate, document, proposal hash, signature or agreement. Every answer is `Cache-Control: no-store`.

| Route | Who | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /api/jobs/{id}/drying-readings?limit=&cursor=` | `jobs_read` (owner, office, and the estimator who made the job, owns its field work or is assigned it) | The job's active readings in the order they were taken (time, then id), at most `limit` (1 to 200, default 100) a page, with `next`, an opaque cursor for the next page (null on the last). Each reading: `id`, `kind`, `roomId` and `roomLabel` (null for a whole-job or outside reading), `takenAt` and `receivedAt` (UTC, microseconds), its fields (`reading`) and, for an air reading, the server's `air` values (GPP, dew point, dew-point depression, vapor pressure, enthalpy). Who made a reading is not in the answer. `canChange` says whether the caller's role may add and void (a hint for the screen; the write routes check again). The job's access is decided first, then the query: any other query, or a cursor that is not a valid position (a real calendar time and a uuid, the format the jobs list uses), is 400 `invalid_request`. A read-only snapshot; writes nothing. |
| `GET /api/jobs/{id}/drying-rooms` | `jobs_read` (owner, office, and the estimator who made the job, owns its field work or is assigned it) | `{jobId, rooms: [{id, label}]}`: the rooms a reading of the job may name, in the room order, by the same rule the reading write checks a room against (one function, `jobRooms`): on a job being edited, the rooms its current field draft's room order lists that have a name (read from the field database with the draft checked to be this job's; 503 `draft_unavailable` when that read fails, 409 `submission_in_progress` while a Submit decides the draft); on a submitted or signed job, those of the snapshot its current draft was committed with. The label is the room's name now. No amounts, readings or people. The job's access is decided first, then the query: any query is 400 `invalid_request` (404 and 410 come first). Read-only; writes nothing. |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/drying-readings` | `scope` (owner; the estimator with the job), on a job being edited, submitted or signed; owning the job's field work is not needed | One air, material or thermal reading: `{id, kind, roomId?, takenAt, note?, ...}` with the kind's own fields only (air: `zone`, `temperatureF` -40 to 150, `relativeHumidity` 1 to 100; material: `material`, `moisture` 0 to 1,000, `unit`, `dryStandard` or null, `affected`; thermal: `deltaTF` -200 to 200, `pattern`, `boundary`). An air value sent by the phone, or any other key, is 400. The body is read (at most 16 KiB, counted as it arrives) but neither refused nor parsed until the job is locked and the caller's access checked: an oversized, malformed or invalid body is 400 only on a job the caller may write (403, 404 and 410 come first). A body that is not UTF-8 (never repaired), text that cannot be stored (an unpaired surrogate) or a time the database could not store as given (a day that does not exist, or an offset beyond 15:59 either way) is 400 too, checked with the body, before the room, the limit or the field database. The same `id` and body again (a lost answer) is 200 with the stored reading, before any other check; the same `id` with another body, or on another job, is 409 `conflict`. A room is chosen by its stable field id: on a job being edited, a room listed in its current field draft (read from the field database, the draft checked to be this job's; 503 `draft_unavailable` when that read fails, 409 `submission_in_progress` while a Submit decides the draft, whether the field draft or the job's pending submission in Postgres says so, checked under the job lock after the replay). On a submitted or signed job, a room of the snapshot its current draft was committed with. Any other room id is 409 `unknown_room`. The room's name then is kept as the reading's label. Outside air has no room. At most 400 active readings per job, counted under the lock after the replay check: 409 `reading_limit`. 201 with the reading; one audit row `drying_reading_added` (reading id and kind). |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/drying-readings/{readingId}/void` | `scope`, as above | `{changeId, expectedState: "active"}` (`expectedState` is required and `active` is its only value): voids the reading once (it stays stored, no longer active, and frees a place under the limit). The job is checked first, then the body, then the reading (ids are compared as lower-case uuids, so the same void in another letter case is the same void): a reading of another job or of no job is 404 `reading_not_found`. A path whose job or reading id is not a uuid matches no route: the proxy answers 401 before any handler (the handler's own 404 for such an id is defense in depth). The same void again is 200 with its receipt `{id, voided: true, changeId, voidedAt}`; another void of a voided reading is 409 `already_voided` (the first void's person and time stay); a `changeId` that already voided another reading is 409 `conflict`. One audit row `drying_reading_voided` (reading id and change id). |

Every route first reads the caller again inside its own transaction: the company still active and the caller still an active member holding the role their sign-in was checked for (in the writes both rows are locked for share, before the job, so a suspension, a disable or a role change waits for the write, and a write that waited sees it). A suspended company is 403 `company_inactive`, as at sign-in; a member disabled or given another role while the request waited is 403 `not_allowed`, a new role never taken on within the request, including for a replay or a request whose field read failed.

Office reads the log and cannot add or void (403 `not_allowed`, after the job's own checks). A job of another company, or another estimator's job they are not assigned, is 404 `not_found` on every route; an archived job is 410 `archived`. A database failure is 503 `drying_unavailable`. The platform operator has no drying log route. "Ready to review?" (S5b-11, [Ready to review?](readiness.md)) reads the drying log's setup reading in its own transaction for the job it has already checked: an active affected air reading or affected material reading taken by now (`web/src/lib/drying/setup.ts`).

## Drying watch (rebuilt app, S5b-10)

Two handlers behind the company's `drying_watch` switch (`requireFlag`, off by default; off: 404 before anything is read), through the job-access function, each re-reading the caller first as above. No new capability key. A signed job only: any other job is 409 `not_signed`. Every answer is `Cache-Control: no-store`. The watch stores only the staff's explicit decisions (`job_coordination.drying_watch`, 0020); its status is worked out on every read from the signed facts, the live drying log and the database's clock (`web/src/lib/drying/watch.ts`, the port of `backend/drying_watch.py`, [Drying watch](../drying-watch.md)).

| Route | Who | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /api/jobs/{id}/drying-watch` | `jobs_read` (owner, office, and the estimator who made the job, owns its field work or is assigned it) | The signed facts: the agreement in force (the latest signed), the drying days and the rooms (stable id and signed name, in signed order) of the snapshot it was signed from; day 1 is the original signing's California day. A signed record without saved drying days answers `{jobId, status: 'unavailable', reason: 'no_plan'}`. Otherwise `{jobId, agreementId, factsHash, revision, status, plannedDays, signedRooms, dayNumber, setupAt, lastReadingAt, nextDueAt, flags, materials, air, missingRooms, airTrends, roomReadings, summary, decisions, suggestedDays, can}`: `status` is `active`, `dry_standard_met`, `marked_dry` or `closed`; `flags` are `missing_dry_standard`, `stalled`, `overdue`, `due_today` and `past_signed_plan` (none once monitoring is not active); `summary` is fixed sentences from those facts (signed room names, no note, person, address or amount); `decisions` are the accepted actions with their reasons, times and who decided (actor id and display name); `suggestedDays` is, once monitoring has finished, the day number of the instant it finished (the closing receipt's time for Mark dry and Close monitoring, the latest eligible reading's time for an automatic dry standard; California days, never above 365), not of the read, for the job close; null while it is active; `can` is the caller's role's hint (Mark dry, Extend, Close; all false once closed, Extend false at 100 extensions). Readings taken in the future and voided readings count for nothing. `factsHash` is the SHA-256 of the agreement, the worked-out facts, the log's version (every reading and void) and the watch's revision. A read-only snapshot; writes nothing. |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/drying-watch/actions` | Mark dry: `scope` or `coordination` (owner, office, the estimator with the job); Extend plan and Close monitoring: `coordination` (owner, office) | `{id, action, factsHash, confirmed: true, reason}`: `action` is `mark_dry`, `extend` or `close`; `reason` is 1 to 500 characters, not blank (line breaks allowed), kept as entered. The caller must hold `scope` or `coordination` before the body is read (403), then the action's own capability (403). The job is locked. The same `id` and body again is 200 with its accepted receipt only (the live watch is not worked out; read it with GET), before any other check; the same `id` with another body is 409 `conflict`. Then: no saved plan 409 `no_plan`; the facts worked out now (the database's clock) differ from `factsHash` 409 `stale_facts` (read the watch again and confirm again); a watch already marked dry or closed 409 `watch_closed`; an extension past the 100th 409 `extension_limit`. Otherwise one receipt is added (action, agreement in force, reason, actor, request id and digest; the database stamps its time) and one audit row `drying_watch_action` (action, request id, revision): 201 with `{receipt, watch}` (the watch worked out after the change). Extend plan adds a note only; the signed days, rooms, prices and agreement never change. Mark dry and Close monitoring close the watch for good. The first action on a job with no coordination row makes the row at coordination revision 0 with empty office notes; otherwise only the watch column changes, never the office notes or their revision. |

Another company's job, or another estimator's job they are not assigned, is 404 `not_found` on both routes; an archived job is 410 `archived`. A database failure is 503 `drying_unavailable`. `GET /api/me` tells the screens whether the switch is on (`features.drying_watch`); both routes check it again inside their own transaction: the action route after taking the company lock the operator's switch writer also takes, so a switch turned off before that lock is 404 with nothing written, and one turned off after it waits for the action; the read route in its snapshot, so it answers as the switch stood when its snapshot began.

## Job close and the estimate history (rebuilt app, S5b-10)

**The history.** Signing (on the phone and through a signing link, the one signing transaction) writes the new agreement's `estimate_history` sample right after the agreement, whatever the `estimate_assist` switch: the price mode, the low end, a range's high end and the low end's labor and materials total, as exact cents read from the agreement's own stored priced result (after any approved named adjustment, multipliers and additions), with the signed rooms' categories as the engine priced them (a room with no category as 1) and the drying days of the snapshot it was signed from. Nothing is priced again. The area stays empty: the accuracy numbers do not read it. The sample is written in a savepoint: one that cannot be built or written is logged with fixed text and no amount, and the signature commits without it; a failure that ends the whole transaction (serialization failure, deadlock, a lost connection, the server shutting down; a statement timeout or a refused row only rolls the sample back) fails the signing as any other would. A signed record whose amount cannot be read gets no sample.

Two handlers behind the company's `estimate_assist` switch (`requireFlag`, off by default; off: 404 before anything is read), through the job-access function, each re-reading the caller first as above. No new capability key. A signed job only: any other job is 409 `not_signed`. Every answer is `Cache-Control: no-store`.

| Route | Who | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /api/jobs/{id}/outcome` | `jobs_read`; the amounts for `coordination` only | `coordination` (owner, office): `{jobId, access: 'full', closed, closedAt, agreementId, revision, close, suggestedDays}`: the agreement in force (the one a close names), the close revision a new close or correction names (0 before the first), the latest close (`{id, revision, agreementId, finalAmount, actualDays, note, baseline: {agreementId, priceMode, low, high}, landed, closedAt, closedBy}`, amounts as dollars and cents), and the drying days to preset: the drying watch's day count once monitoring has finished, with the `drying_watch` switch on (`source: 'watch'`), otherwise the signed plan's days (`source: 'plan'`), or null. Anyone else with the job (the estimator): `{jobId, access: 'status', closed, closedAt}` only, from a query that reads nothing else of the close. A read-only snapshot. |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/close` | `coordination` (owner, office) | `{id, expectedAgreementId, expectedRevision, finalAmount, actualDays, note}`: `finalAmount` is dollars and cents as text, $0.01 to $2,000,000.00 (stored as cents); `actualDays` 0 to 365, or null or blank for none (zero is kept); `note` at most 300 characters. The job is locked. The same `id` and body again is 200 with its original receipt, even after a correction; the same `id` with another body is 409 `conflict`. Then: the agreement in force must be `expectedAgreementId` (409 `stale_agreement`) and the job's close revision `expectedRevision` (409 `stale_revision`). The final invoice is compared with the job's first signed estimate (owner decision Q1, option A): the original agreement's history sample, written now from its signed record when it is missing; when its signed amount cannot be proven, 409 `no_signed_amount` and nothing is written. Where it landed: a range is `inside` from low to high, both included, `below` under the low and `above` over the high; one price is `inside` at or under it and `above` over it. The close is a new row with the next close revision, the database stamping its time; earlier versions stay. 201 with `{close}`; one audit row `job_closed` (close id and revision; no amount). |

Another company's job, or another estimator's job they are not assigned, is 404 `not_found` on both routes; an archived job is 410 `archived`. The estimator's 403 on the close comes after the job's own checks. Both routes read the `estimate_assist` switch again inside their own transaction (the close after the company lock the operator's switch writer also takes), so a switch turned off while a request waited is 404 with nothing written. Nothing here changes a signed record, a document, a signing link or a price. `GET /api/me` tells the screens whether the switch is on (`features.estimate_assist`).
