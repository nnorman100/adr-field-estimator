# Access matrix: Drying log

Part of [Access matrix](../ACCESS.md).

## Drying log (rebuilt app, S5b-10)

`web/src/lib/drying/`, three handlers, `memberRoute`, through the job-access function (`jobAccess`). No new capability key and no feature switch: reading uses `jobs_read`, adding and voiding use `scope`. The company is the caller's own from the sign-in; the path names the job (and the reading) and nothing else. The readings sit beside the job, never in its field draft or signed record: adding or voiding one changes no job revision, field revision, estimate, document, proposal hash, signature or agreement. Every answer is `Cache-Control: no-store`.

| Route | Who | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /api/jobs/{id}/drying-readings?limit=&cursor=` | `jobs_read` (owner, office, and the estimator who made the job, owns its field work or is assigned it) | The job's active readings in the order they were taken (time, then id), at most `limit` (1 to 200, default 100) a page, with `next`, an opaque cursor for the next page (null on the last). Each reading: `id`, `kind`, `roomId` and `roomLabel` (null for a whole-job or outside reading), `takenAt` and `receivedAt` (UTC, microseconds), its fields (`reading`) and, for an air reading, the server's `air` values (GPP, dew point, dew-point depression, vapor pressure, enthalpy). Who made a reading is not in the answer. `canChange` says whether the caller's role may add and void (a hint for the screen; the write routes check again). Any other query is 400. A read-only snapshot; writes nothing. |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/drying-readings` | `scope` (owner; the estimator with the job), on a job being edited, submitted or signed; owning the job's field work is not needed | One air, material or thermal reading: `{id, kind, roomId?, takenAt, note?, ...}` with the kind's own fields only (air: `zone`, `temperatureF` -40 to 150, `relativeHumidity` 1 to 100; material: `material`, `moisture` 0 to 1,000, `unit`, `dryStandard` or null, `affected`; thermal: `deltaTF` -200 to 200, `pattern`, `boundary`). An air value sent by the phone, or any other key, is 400. The job is locked and its access checked first, then the body. The same `id` and body again (a lost answer) is 200 with the stored reading, before any other check; the same `id` with another body, or on another job, is 409 `conflict`. A room is chosen by its stable field id: on a job being edited, a room listed in its current field draft (read from the field database, the draft checked to be this job's; 503 `draft_unavailable` when that read fails, 409 `submission_in_progress` while a Submit decides the draft); on a submitted or signed job, a room of the snapshot its current draft was committed with. Any other room id is 409 `unknown_room`. The room's name then is kept as the reading's label. Outside air has no room. At most 400 active readings per job, counted under the lock after the replay check: 409 `reading_limit`. 201 with the reading; one audit row `drying_reading_added` (reading id and kind). |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/drying-readings/{readingId}/void` | `scope`, as above | `{changeId}`: voids the reading once (it stays stored, no longer active, and frees a place under the limit). The job is checked first, then the body, then the reading: a reading of another job, of no job or with an id that is not a uuid is 404 `reading_not_found`. The same void again is 200 with its receipt `{id, voided: true, changeId, voidedAt}`; another void of a voided reading is 409 `already_voided` (the first void's person and time stay); a `changeId` that already voided another reading is 409 `conflict`. One audit row `drying_reading_voided` (reading id and change id). |

Office reads the log and cannot add or void (403 `not_allowed`, after the job's own checks). A job of another company, or another estimator's job they are not assigned, is 404 `not_found` on every route; an archived job is 410 `archived`. A database failure is 503 `drying_unavailable`. The platform operator has no drying log route. "Ready to review?" (S5b-11, [Ready to review?](readiness.md)) reads the drying log's setup reading in its own transaction for the job it has already checked: an active affected air reading or affected material reading taken by now (`web/src/lib/drying/setup.ts`).
