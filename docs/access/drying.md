# Access matrix: Drying log

Part of [Access matrix](../ACCESS.md).

## Drying log (rebuilt app, S5b-10)

`web/src/lib/drying/`, three handlers, `memberRoute`, through the job-access function (`jobAccess`). No new capability key and no feature switch: reading uses `jobs_read`, adding and voiding use `scope`. The company is the caller's own from the sign-in; the path names the job (and the reading) and nothing else. The readings sit beside the job, never in its field draft or signed record: adding or voiding one changes no job revision, field revision, estimate, document, proposal hash, signature or agreement. Every answer is `Cache-Control: no-store`.

| Route | Who | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /api/jobs/{id}/drying-readings?limit=&cursor=` | `jobs_read` (owner, office, and the estimator who made the job, owns its field work or is assigned it) | The job's active readings in the order they were taken (time, then id), at most `limit` (1 to 200, default 100) a page, with `next`, an opaque cursor for the next page (null on the last). Each reading: `id`, `kind`, `roomId` and `roomLabel` (null for a whole-job or outside reading), `takenAt` and `receivedAt` (UTC, microseconds), its fields (`reading`) and, for an air reading, the server's `air` values (GPP, dew point, dew-point depression, vapor pressure, enthalpy). Who made a reading is not in the answer. `canChange` says whether the caller's role may add and void (a hint for the screen; the write routes check again). The job's access is decided first, then the query: any other query, or a cursor that is not a valid position (a real calendar time and a uuid, the format the jobs list uses), is 400 `invalid_request`. A read-only snapshot; writes nothing. |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/drying-readings` | `scope` (owner; the estimator with the job), on a job being edited, submitted or signed; owning the job's field work is not needed | One air, material or thermal reading: `{id, kind, roomId?, takenAt, note?, ...}` with the kind's own fields only (air: `zone`, `temperatureF` -40 to 150, `relativeHumidity` 1 to 100; material: `material`, `moisture` 0 to 1,000, `unit`, `dryStandard` or null, `affected`; thermal: `deltaTF` -200 to 200, `pattern`, `boundary`). An air value sent by the phone, or any other key, is 400. The body is read (at most 16 KiB, counted as it arrives) but neither refused nor parsed until the job is locked and the caller's access checked: an oversized, malformed or invalid body is 400 only on a job the caller may write (403, 404 and 410 come first). A body that is not UTF-8 (never repaired), text that cannot be stored (an unpaired surrogate) or a time the database could not store as given (a day that does not exist, or an offset beyond 15:59 either way) is 400 too, checked with the body, before the room, the limit or the field database. The same `id` and body again (a lost answer) is 200 with the stored reading, before any other check; the same `id` with another body, or on another job, is 409 `conflict`. A room is chosen by its stable field id: on a job being edited, a room listed in its current field draft (read from the field database, the draft checked to be this job's; 503 `draft_unavailable` when that read fails, 409 `submission_in_progress` while a Submit decides the draft, whether the field draft or the job's pending submission in Postgres says so, checked under the job lock after the replay). On a submitted or signed job, a room of the snapshot its current draft was committed with. Any other room id is 409 `unknown_room`. The room's name then is kept as the reading's label. Outside air has no room. At most 400 active readings per job, counted under the lock after the replay check: 409 `reading_limit`. 201 with the reading; one audit row `drying_reading_added` (reading id and kind). |
| `POST /api/jobs/{id}/drying-readings/{readingId}/void` | `scope`, as above | `{changeId, expectedState: "active"}` (`expectedState` is required and `active` is its only value): voids the reading once (it stays stored, no longer active, and frees a place under the limit). The job is checked first, then the body, then the reading (ids are compared as lower-case uuids, so the same void in another letter case is the same void): a reading of another job or of no job is 404 `reading_not_found`. A path whose job or reading id is not a uuid matches no route: the proxy answers 401 before any handler (the handler's own 404 for such an id is defense in depth). The same void again is 200 with its receipt `{id, voided: true, changeId, voidedAt}`; another void of a voided reading is 409 `already_voided` (the first void's person and time stay); a `changeId` that already voided another reading is 409 `conflict`. One audit row `drying_reading_voided` (reading id and change id). |

Every route first reads the caller again inside its own transaction: the company still active and the caller still an active member holding the role their sign-in was checked for (in the writes both rows are locked for share, before the job, so a suspension, a disable or a role change waits for the write, and a write that waited sees it). A suspended company is 403 `company_inactive`, as at sign-in; a member disabled or given another role while the request waited is 403 `not_allowed`, a new role never taken on within the request, including for a replay or a request whose field read failed.

Office reads the log and cannot add or void (403 `not_allowed`, after the job's own checks). A job of another company, or another estimator's job they are not assigned, is 404 `not_found` on every route; an archived job is 410 `archived`. A database failure is 503 `drying_unavailable`. The platform operator has no drying log route. "Ready to review?" (S5b-11, [Ready to review?](readiness.md)) reads the drying log's setup reading in its own transaction for the job it has already checked: an active affected air reading or affected material reading taken by now (`web/src/lib/drying/setup.ts`).
