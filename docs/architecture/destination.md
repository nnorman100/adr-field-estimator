## Destination (accepted, not built yet)

Source: the rebuild plan (`dry-standard-replan` report, sections 3, 7 and 8) under the Technology
Standard Stack, updated by the owner's decision to build the clean-start rebuild in dedicated
project `dryscope` (`570228742694`), with no data migration from `alert-disaster-field-2026`.
The `test` environment is in `dryscope` (`us-central1`) and `live` gets its own project before S8 (D-018); throwaway test data stays in CI
and the `test` environment's smoke-test company, with no hidden test company on `live` (`live`
checks are read-only). Every row is a **Gap** unless marked Partial or Partly; the stage is the one that closes it.

Stage plan (owner, Nov 9, 2026): S5 is split into S5a (core records and exactly-once handoff,
before S7 live voice) and S5b (the remaining workflows). S8 is reduced to go-live: the live
environment, domain and web address, and pointing users at the new app; the owner retires the old
app later.
