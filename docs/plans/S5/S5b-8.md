# S5 plan, step S5b-8

Part of [the S5 plan](../S5.md), section 5, "Ordered steps".

**S5b-8 Proposals and approvals on a job (DB job)** (pricing plan S5b-3): `job_price_approvals`; estimator proposes named adjustments, one custom multiplier per line, and hourly, unit or flat additions; the owner approves (new key `additions_approve`, today an owner-role check); approval bound to the job revision and price hash; any edit clears it; flag `priced_extras` joins the known flags; the migration also adds the submission and agreement kind `revision` for S5b-9 (added beside `original` and `change_order`, nothing renamed or dropped). Tests: `spec.md` 6.1 checks; a `revision` names its parent agreement. Needs: S5b-7, the lane.

## Corrections

- **2026-11-20, owner decisions D1 to D5 ("all A, 3× cap").** (D1) The estimator proposes and the owner approves after Finish, on the submitted job. A proposal and its approval are bound to the committed submission of the job's current draft, `jobs.revision` and the pinned version's content hash; a proposal or approval change after the estimate is made goes through Back to editing and Finish, so `job_estimates` is unchanged. (D2) The estimator types a custom multiplier for one priced item in one room, or one job-wide item: a surcharge only, above 1 and at most 3, with two decimal places; the owner approves that exact value; one per line, and a second proposal for the same line is refused; bracket-derived lines are eligible. (D3) Hourly, unit and flat additions are all rows in `job_price_approvals`; the hourly rate is taken from the price table's `extras` item when proposed and saved on the row at approval. (D4) At most one approved named adjustment per job (S5b-7's discount or charge), and a newly approved one replaces it. (D5) With `priced_extras` off, every propose and approve route answers 404, the screens hide, and the estimate ignores approvals. The step ships as three pull requests: migration `0019_price_approvals` with its database tests, then the server, then the screens.
