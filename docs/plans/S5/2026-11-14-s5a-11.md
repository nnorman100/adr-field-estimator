# S5 plan correction: 2026-11-14, S5a-11

Part of [the S5 plan](../S5.md), section 12, "Corrections".

- **2026-11-14, S5a-11.** Sign on the phone (`web/src/lib/agreements/`, `web/src/app/api/jobs/[jobId]/agreements/`, the signing part of Review before signing; `docs/ACCESS.md` "Signing on the phone") uses no new capability key and no migration, with these choices the plan left open. (a) The proposal bytes, now format `dryscope-proposal-v2`, hold the company's `authorization_text` and `payment_terms_text` exactly as entered, after the price, so the signature's proposal hash binds them; the estimate passes both to the engine's setup check, which lists an empty one ("Start-work authorization", "Payment terms") and makes the documents not signable. A v1 proposal, made before, is never signable. (b) The signature goes through the photo receipt as `kind=signature`, needs `sign`, and needs a current unsigned estimate (the job submitted, its current record priced at its current pin); it is stored under `signatures/` in the job's path, create-only, and a retry of the same bytes is the same receipt even after signing. (c) `POST /api/jobs/{id}/agreements` checks, under the job's row lock: the same id and body (else 409 `conflict`), not signed (`already_signed`), submitted, the setup check on the company's current profile (`company_setup_missing` with its list), the estimate named is the job's current one (`estimate_superseded`, which is how a new estimate before signing voids the old one), the proposal hash, not needing review, the stored documents signable with both texts in the proposal (`not_signable`; an estimate priced before the texts were filled in is re-priced through Back to editing and Finish), and the signature received. The body carries the documents-confirmed box as `documentsConfirmed: true` and the signer's name, trimmed. (d) The agreement's documents bytes are a signed record, `dryscope-signed-v1`: the signer's name, the server time (the database clock at signing to the millisecond, the same instant as `signed_at`), how (`device` with the presenting technician's id and name, their sign-in name or else their sign-in email; S5a-11b's `link` kind carries the link id instead), the signature's id and hash, the proposal exactly as stored (its canonical text is the hashed bytes, checked when written and when read), and the estimate's itemized claim, letter, photo log and claim. (e) `GET` on the same path (`jobs_read`) reads the signed record from those bytes; the review screen opens a signed job read-only from it. Signing is on the review screen after the documents-confirmed box: the customer's name, a pointer-events signature pad, and Sign; success shows only after the agreement's answer, and both requests keep their ids for a retry. "Email me a copy" is not shown (J7). (f) After signing, the existing refusals hold (customer, claim, photo, submission, Back to editing, estimate) and a new signature is refused; a full-size original of a registered photo is still accepted, as S5a-5 allows, and changes nothing signed.
