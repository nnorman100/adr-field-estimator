# S5 plan correction: 2026-11-14, S5a-9c

Part of [the S5 plan](../S5.md), section 12, "Corrections".

- **2026-11-14, S5a-9c.** The photo audit log is grouped by the company's own `photo_log_grouping` (`readCompanyDocuments` now returns it from the stored settings, read through the S5a-4b schema; the layout and clauses stay at their neutral defaults for S5a-9b; settings that do not read refuse the estimate with 503 `settings_unreadable` and store nothing). By `room` the rows and bytes are exactly S5a-9's. By `shot_tag` the rows are ordered in the field map's shot-tag order (overview, source, surface, meter, contents, equipment), photos with no tag last, and each row carries an `album` heading (`SHOT_NAMES`, "Not tagged" for the last); ids, hashes, rooms and tags are the snapshot's in both, and Review lists each album under its heading with the photos numbered straight through. Note (2026-11-14): within a room or an album the photos keep the snapshot's order (sorted by photo id), which is not capture order; the snapshot holds no capture time, so real capture order needs a capture time recorded on the photo and carried into the snapshot, and is follow-up work. The grouping is fixed when the estimate is made: an estimate already stored keeps the log it was made with, and a changed setting shows on the next estimate, as with the pinned prices.
