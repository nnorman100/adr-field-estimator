# S5 plan, step S5a-11

Part of [the S5 plan](../S5.md), section 5, "Ordered steps".

**S5a-11 Sign on the phone.** UI: signature pad, signer name, "Email me a copy" hidden until email exists (J7); success only after the server receipt; the signed job opens read-only from stored bytes. BE: signature image through the receipt route (`kind=signature`); `POST /api/jobs/{id}/agreements` (client id): one transaction locks the job, checks the estimate is the latest for the current submission, the pin unchanged since the estimate, the proposal hash, the caller's `sign`, and that no agreement exists; inserts the agreement; sets the job `signed`. Owner sign-off before signing follows J2. DB no. Tests: the same signature twice returns one receipt and one agreement; a changed body with the same id 409; a second agreement refused; after signing, every field write, upload, submission and estimate outside S5b-9's revision and change-order paths is refused and the stored bytes are unchanged; a publish or engine change never alters a signed job's documents. Needs: S5a-9, S5a-5. **Since 2026-11-13 (the owner chose all four options)**, customer approval has four parts, each the same for every company and worded per company: (1) **On-site phone signing**, as above. (2) **A remote signing link** for a customer who is not there, sent by text or email: S5a-11b (was S5b-3). (3) **A locked signed record**: the agreement row (insert-only, S5a-7) and the signed page state who signed (signer name), when (server time), and how (on the technician's phone, with the technician who presented it, or through a signing link, with the link id); any change, before or after signing (adding, removing or reducing a room, item or quantity), makes a revised estimate that supersedes the previous one, voids the old estimate and any open link, and applies only once the customer signs it; a signed agreement, its bytes and its evidence never change, and after signing the revised estimate is built and signed through S5b-9, which records the agreement it supersedes. A change order (S5b-9) stays the path for work added while the job is in progress, and is signed too. (4) **Start-work authorization and payment terms on the signed page**: the company's `authorization_text` and `payment_terms_text` (S5a-4b) print exactly as entered above the signature, inside the proposal bytes whose hash the signature binds; `companySetupMissing` lists them, and signing is refused with the setup check's answer until both are set. DryScope ships no cancellation notice or emergency waiver wording of its own until the lawyer review items in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (Product rules §4C) are answered; until then a company may put its own wording in its authorization text. Tests add: the signed page shows signer, time and device or link; the authorization and payment terms print exactly as entered and are inside the hashed bytes; signing without them is refused; a new estimate before signing voids the old one for signing. Needs add S5a-4b.
