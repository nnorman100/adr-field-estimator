# S5 plan correction: 2026-11-15, S5a-11b

Part of [the S5 plan](../S5.md), section 12, "Corrections".

- **2026-11-15, S5a-11b.** Signing links and document links (`web/src/lib/links/`, migration `0014_signing_links`, the Send to the homeowner and Share the signed documents parts of Review before signing, the `/sign` and `/documents` pages; `docs/ACCESS.md` "Signing links and document links") use no new capability key, with these choices the plan left open. (a) A new access rule `linkRoute` reads no sign-in: the token travels only in the `X-Link-Token` header, the page address carries it in its fragment (never sent to a server), the database keeps only its SHA-256, and two lookups on the 0004 pattern (`signing_link_company`, `document_link_company`) find the token's company before everything else runs inside that company under forced row-level security. (b) The link signs through S5a-11's one transaction (shared as `signInTransaction` with a link claim checked under the job's row lock and consumed with the insert), as the technician who made it, rebuilt on every use from their current membership, role and the company's switches (`sign` and price books on, company active); the estimate and proposal hash come from the link, never the browser. The agreement's new nullable `signing_link_id` is set exactly when the signer kind is `link` (a check) and names a link for the same estimate (a composite foreign key); the signed record says `signed_with: { kind: 'link', link_id }`. (c) Voiding is found when the link is next used and then written (`voided`): the job back to editing or with a newer submission, a newer estimate, an estimate needing review, or a job signed another way; signing checks it again under the job's row lock. A company setting emptied after the link was made refuses signing for now (`link_not_ready`) without voiding. (d) The homeowner gives their name and role (homeowner, adjuster or other, `spec.md` 2.5) and ticks two boxes (documents reviewed; signing and receiving the record electronically) that the request must carry; "Email me a copy" stays hidden (J7). The role is kept in the agreement's new nullable `signer_role` (a check: set for every link signature; signing on the phone does not ask it) and in the signed record as `signer.role`, optional in `dryscope-signed-v1` so records made before read unchanged, and the signed pages show it. (e) Both homeowner pages show the photos: each line of the photo log opens its photo on request (one request each, inside the link's limits) through `GET /api/sign/photos/{id}` or `GET /api/documents/photos/{id}` (the homeowner's set only), which serve only a photo of the submitted record behind the documents, on the link's own job; any other id, of that job, another job or another company, is the same unavailable answer, and nothing lists photos. (f) Document links are made at signing: the signing transaction, on the phone and through a link, makes the agreement's two links (the homeowner's set and the adjuster's) as the person who signs and answers their tokens once to whoever signed (the technician's phone, or the homeowner's page), in the answer that signed only: the same signing request again answers the same agreement and no token, and replaces none. One active link per agreement and set. The owner or office (`delivery_resend`) revokes one and may make it again, replacing a working one; whoever may sign makes one again only when the set has no working link (409 `link_active`). Replacing a token revokes the one it replaces, so it needs `delivery_resend` wherever it happens: a retried create (the same id and body) answers a new token to the owner or office only (403 otherwise), so making a link never revokes one for someone who may not revoke. A document link works while its maker holds `sign` or `delivery_resend` with the job. (g) Limits: 120 requests a minute per client address, held in memory per server instance as a keyed hash (an address belongs to no company, so it has no table); 60 requests a minute per link and 10 signing attempts per link (a signature and a sign request each), counted in the link's row in a transaction of their own; a link's signature image is at most 1 MiB. (h) A retried create with the same link id replaces the token hash and answers a new token while the link is pending; a newer link revokes the job's older pending one.
