# S5 plan, step S5b-7

Part of [the S5 plan](../S5.md), section 5, "Ordered steps".

**S5b-7 Named adjustments** (pricing plan S5b-2). Needs: S4-8. **Detail since 2026-11-13 (G4):** each company defines its own adjustments in its price table: a name (up to 60 characters), a percent or a fixed amount, and whether it is a discount or a charge; none are seeded. On the proposal an applied one prints as arithmetic: a discount as "{price before} − {name} {amount} = {price}", a charge as "{price before} + {name} {amount} = {price}". It applies after price-point rounding (pricing S4-4e) and the result is not rounded again; how it meets tax is shown in a worked example and confirmed with the company's accountant (M9). Tests: a percent discount, an amount discount and a charge as worked examples, the printed arithmetic (minus for a discount, plus for a charge), a discount on a rounded price, no adjustment without the owner's approval (S5b-8).
