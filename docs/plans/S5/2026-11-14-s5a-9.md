# S5 plan correction: 2026-11-14, S5a-9

Part of [the S5 plan](../S5.md), section 12, "Corrections".

- **2026-11-14, S5a-9.** The estimate routes and Review before signing (`web/src/lib/estimates/`, `web/src/app/api/jobs/[jobId]/estimate/`, `web/src/app/(auth)/sign-in/[address]/jobs/[jobId]/review/`, `docs/ACCESS.md` "Estimate and review") use no new capability key and no migration, with these choices the plan left open. (a) Beside `POST`, a `GET` on the same path reads the current estimate, because `docs/spec.md` 2.3 lets every job reader read the review (`jobs_read`, office included) while only `audit` makes it; both sit behind `requireFlag('price_books')`. (b) The estimate is the committed submission of the job's current draft priced from the job's newest pin; with no pin, the company's current version is pinned in the same transaction, under the job's row lock, so a refusal (any of them) rolls the pin back and writes nothing. A record whose scope was not confirmed is refused (`scope_not_confirmed`, the spec 2.3 rule "the audit is refused until scope is confirmed"), and so is one with no written summary (`summary_missing`; the documents need it, J1). A version whose engine version or hash is not this server's is refused (`prices_unreadable`), never priced by other formulas. (c) The proposal bytes are canonical JSON, format `dryscope-proposal-v1`: the heading "[Company] · Official proposal", the letterhead lines that are set, customer and property, the engine's findings, scope and clauses, the price (each line only when the table's `show_line_breakdown` is on) and the basis (submission, snapshot hash, price version, content hash, engine version). The review shows the proposal from these stored bytes. (d) The record knows a photo's room, shot tag and stored hash but no capture time, size or meter reading, so the engine's photo log takes those keys as optional and prints a photo with no `ocr_status` as a supporting photo that never holds up signing; it also carries the photo's `sha256` and `shot_tag`. The log is grouped by room in the record's room order, a photo naming no room of the record last as "No room recorded". (e) The staff-only notes are the engine's written summary, suggestions, company fields still missing and pricing checks, now returned by `priceEstimate` as `documents.staff_notes`; the quantity-override note is in the answer only for `quantity_notes_read`. Field map v1 records no override, so that note is empty for every submitted job today; the filter is tested with an engine override (`web/tests/unit/estimate-proposal.test.ts`). (f) The one document reader is `readCompanyDocuments` (`web/src/lib/profile/documents.ts`): the S5a-4 profile, with the S5a-4b letterhead fields empty and the settings at their neutral defaults. Its settings type admits only the defaults (itemized, no clauses, by room), so S5a-9b and S5a-9c widen it and the compiler finds every place that prints. (g) The documents-confirmed box is on the screen only (for those who hold `sign`, and only when the documents are signable); S5a-11 sends it with the signature. Pricing is an explicit "Price this job" button, not a side effect of opening the page, and Finish and review links to the review once a job is recorded. (h) Of the pricing plan's section 6 "pins and signing", this step tests the pin (version 1 still prices a new record of the job after version 2 is published, and an estimate already made is unchanged byte for byte); "an estimator cannot reprice" and "reprice clears the audit" belong to the reprice route (S5b-6) and the signing checks to S5a-11. (i) The customer, the claim and the property address stay editable while the job is submitted (J3). A save advances the job's revision but not its record, whose snapshot holds them as they were at Finish and cannot change, and `job_estimates` holds one row per record and pin, so no migration is needed and none is made. The stored priced result carries `job_revision`, the job revision whose details its documents state; at the first price that is the job's revision if the record's address, customer and claim equal the job's current ones, and empty otherwise (Submit stores no revision, so a save between Finish and the first price is found by comparing them, only then). From then on only the revision decides: when the job's revision is newer, or `job_revision` is empty, the estimate needs review. Neither route refuses: both answer the stored estimate (`POST` 200, nothing written) with the stored prices and the documents rebuilt from the record with the current address, customer and claim and the company's current profile at the pinned version, with `needsReview: true` and not signable. The record was already priced, so the rebuild applies none of the first price's checks (scope confirmed, summary written) and nothing about the details refuses: when a detail the documents need is now missing (the property address cleared; the year built and rooms are the record's), the answer is the stored prices with `missingDetails` in plain words ("Property address is missing"), no documents and no proposal, and the review screen lists it; once the address is saved again the documents are rebuilt, still needing review. A save that puts the old values back still needs review. Review at the new details is Back to editing and then Finish, which make a new record and a new estimate at the same pin; the review screen says so. A later step could allow review in place, without a new record, with a migration (an estimate per job revision), if owners ask for it. S5a-11's signing refuses an estimate that needs review.
