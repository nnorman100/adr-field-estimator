# S5 plan, step S5b-9

Part of [the S5 plan](../S5.md), section 5, "Ordered steps".

**S5b-9 Change orders and revised estimates after signing** (pricing plan S5b-4). UI: change order on a signed job. BE: a change-order draft, submission, estimate and agreement through the S5a path with `kind` and `parent` (columns from S5a-7, so DB no); prices only the added work; minimum and trip charge off unless selected (Q15); actual drying days billed here (M4); who may create per J6. Tests: original agreement, hash and evidence byte-for-byte unchanged; two concurrent change orders cannot both build on a stale parent; not counted as a separate job in accuracy. Needs: S5b-8. **Since 2026-11-13 (owner):** after signing, any change (adding, removing or reducing a room, item or quantity) can be made as a revised estimate: a draft from the signed job, then a submission, estimate and agreement through the S5a path with kind `revision` and `parent` the agreement it supersedes (kind from S5b-8's migration, so DB no here). It prices the whole job from the job's pin, prints "Revised {date}: was {signed price}" (S5b-6), and applies only once the customer signs it (on the phone or through a link, S5a-11b); until then the signed agreement stays in force. The superseded agreement, its bytes, hash and evidence never change; the newest signed agreement is the one in force. Who may create it follows J6. Tests add: removing a room and reducing a quantity after signing each give a revised estimate at the lower price; the superseded agreement byte-for-byte unchanged; a revision is not in force until signed; a revision and a change order cannot both build on the same stale parent.
