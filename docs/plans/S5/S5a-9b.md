# S5 plan, step S5a-9b

Part of [the S5 plan](../S5.md), section 5, "Ordered steps".

**S5a-9b Proposal layout options (added 2026-11-13, D1 to D3).** UI: Review shows the Proposal in the company's chosen layout; Company settings (S5a-4b) explains each option. FE: the review screen renders both layouts. BE: `makeDocuments` takes the document settings. **Itemized** (the default) is S5a-9's proposal. **Letter** is one plain letter: "Area inspected" lists every room on the job by name, a room with no lines included, with no label added; "Findings" has the mandated age sentence first, then one paragraph per room from that room's findings note (S5a-10); "Recommendations" has the mandated testing and protective-equipment sentences first, then per room: the room's prep tasks, each marked with an asterisk, then each line as "{item}: up to {quantity} {unit}" from S4-4d's per-room quantities; then the company's clauses; then the standard clauses and the price line. In both layouts the line prices follow the price table's `show_line_breakdown` (off: the work is listed without prices and only the total is shown; on: each line shows its price); the itemized claim always shows line prices. Company clauses are added text only: they never replace, reorder or remove a mandated clause, and the mandated clauses keep their exact text and conditions in both layouts. The heading stays "[Company] · Official proposal" (ARCHITECTURE Product rules §2), and the letterhead prints the S5a-4b fields that are set. DB no. FS none (the room fields are S5a-10's). Tests: exact clause text in both layouts, before and after 1978; a room with no lines appears under Area inspected by name only, with its recorded findings under Findings; prep tasks print under their room; line prices hidden with the setting off and the total still shown; a company clause prints exactly as entered and the standard clauses are all still there; a company with no settings gets proposal bytes identical to S5a-9's; one company's settings never reach another company's documents. Needs: S5a-9, S5a-4b, S5a-10, S4-4d.
