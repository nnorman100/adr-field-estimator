# S5 plan correction: 2026-11-13, estimates review, owner-approved

Part of [the S5 plan](../S5.md), section 12, "Corrections".

- **2026-11-13, estimates review, owner-approved.** A review of 31 real estimates from a restoration company compared how a working company writes, prices and gets approval for an estimate with what S5a-9 to S5a-11 build. The owner accepted every recommendation on 2026-11-13 and added that the product serves many companies: each finding becomes a setting or template that **any company** configures, with neutral defaults, and no company's name, wording, prices or defaults go into product code, defaults or plans (each company's own values live only in its own Company settings and price table). These corrections land before S5a-9 starts. (a) **D4, letterhead fields and document settings**: new step S5a-4b (a database job). (b) **D1 to D3, the proposal**: new step S5a-9b gives every company a choice of the itemized proposal or a plain letter (area inspected, per-room findings, per-room recommendations with "up to" quantities), shown or hidden line prices (the existing `show_line_breakdown`), the company's own clauses (for example a standard closing paragraph) and prep tasks per room; S5a-9 itself is unchanged except that it reads the settings with neutral defaults. (c) **D7, photo log**: new step S5a-9c groups the Photo audit log by room or by shot tag. (d) **S5a-10** carries per-room item quantities (pricing plan S4-4d, G1), prep tasks and a per-room findings note in field map v2. (e) **S5a-11, customer approval, all four parts** (owner, 2026-11-13): on-site phone signing; a remote signing link sent by text or email, which moves S5b-3 into S5a as S5a-11b; a locked signed record (who, when, link or device), with any change, before or after signing, becoming a revised estimate that needs a new signature (after signing through S5b-9, which leaves the signed agreement unchanged; change orders stay the path for added work); and start-work authorization and payment terms on the signed page, worded per company. This replaces the review's open question D5 (how customers authorize work today). (f) **S5b-6** heads a revised estimate "Revised {date}" and, with the company's setting on, prints the previous price (D6). (g) **S5b-7** detail: a company's named discounts and charges, printed as arithmetic (G4). (h) **Wording, W1 to W5**: the mandated California text and the standard clauses stay exactly as in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `web/src/engine/documents.ts`. W1 (the pre-1978 Findings sentence differs from the sentence the reviewed estimates use) and W4 (the licence label, "CSLB #" or "CA License #") go to the lawyer review list (ARCHITECTURE Product rules §4C), with the California right to cancel and the emergency-repair waiver. W2 (one protective-equipment sentence, the engine's), W3 (the full exclusions NOTE on every job, demolition or not) and W5 (the pre-test notice as its own clause) keep today's text and placement. (i) **D8** (equipment lines on remediation proposals) and the pricing plan's G5 (contingent items) stay open; G7 (a second price on one estimate) is out of scope. (j) Counts: S5a gains S5a-4b, S5a-9b, S5a-9c and S5a-11b; S5 has seven database jobs instead of six (S5a-4b added; S5b-3 is now S5a-11b); section 6 gains wave E2, and S5a-12 takes the link routes and the real-phone homeowner signing check from S5b-12.
