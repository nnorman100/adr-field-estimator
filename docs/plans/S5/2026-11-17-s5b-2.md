# S5 plan correction: 2026-11-17, S5b-2

Part of [the S5 plan](../S5.md), section 12, "Corrections".

- **2026-11-17, S5b-2.** Archive, restore and copy, server and field-rules slice (`web/src/lib/jobs/archive.ts`, `archive-store.ts`, the routes under `docs/access/jobs.md` "Archive, restore and copy"; owner decision 2026-11-16: the screens, Archive, Archived drafts with Restore and Copy, follow in S5b-2b), with no new capability key and no migration, and these choices the plan left open. (a) Archive and restore carry the job `revision` and a `changeId`, as the customer save; the same change again is the same answer. "Unsigned draft" is a job in `draft` or `submitted`; a signed one is refused (409 `job_signed`). (b) Archive waits while a Submit of the job is unsettled (a pending row, or a decided row whose field marker is not written yet): 409 `submission_in_progress`, so no fence, release or marker ever meets an archived draft. (c) The field draft's `archived` state is set after the Postgres commit, in a Firestore transaction with `archiveRevision` (the job revision), so a slower archive never lands after a newer restore; restore changes the state only of a draft that is archived. The field rules' logic is unchanged: a client already writes only while `state` is `editing`, so `archived` refuses every client write; the rules file's comment names the new state and key. (d) Restore finds the status from the job's submissions (S5a-2 (f)): `submitted` when the current draft has a committed one, else `draft`. Archive and restore each move the job revision, so a signing link of a submitted job is voided and stays voided after restore, and its estimate is made again. (e) "New client ids" are the new job's and first draft's ids, made on the phone; the copy keeps the signed record's room ids, which are scoped by the draft. (f) "Transcripts and approvals cleared": the field map holds no transcript (a Live walkthrough's is never kept in a draft) and one approval, `scopeConfirmed`, which the copy leaves out, as the old app did. The copy takes the signed record's property address, field map and rooms from its snapshot, and not the customer, the claim or the photo references (the photos are the signed job's evidence). (g) The owner's "Archived drafts" list is `GET /api/jobs?scope=archived` (`jobs_archive`). (h) As on every job route, a role check comes before the job is looked at, so a role that lacks a route's key is answered 403, not 410, for an archived job.
- **2026-11-17, S5b-2b.** The screens, on the routes above, with no new route, key or field view. (a) Archive is the owner's (`jobs_archive`) button under a draft or submitted job's screens, behind a native modal confirm dialog whose safe choice, Keep the job, has the focus; a signed job shows no Archive. (b) Archived drafts is the owner's third jobs list (`/field?list=archived`), each row with Restore; a restored job leaves the list, with a link to open it. (c) A job the server answers 410 (an old link, or one just archived on screen) shows that it is archived and kept, with the way to Archived drafts for the owner; screens of it already open stay, read only, with what the person changed and did not save. (d) Copy to a new draft is one tap on a signed job (`jobs_copy`); its new job and draft ids are kept on the page until the server answers (`src/lib/field/pending.ts`), so a lost answer is sent again with the same ids, and the copy then opens as a new job does. Archive and restore keep their change id the same way while unsettled (no answer, or 5xx such as `draft_unavailable`), per job, so their Try again stays on the job's screens and above every jobs list of the owner, also once the job has left the list it was sent from.
- **2026-11-17, S5b-2c.** Fixes to the S5b-2b screens before real users (owner decision 2026-11-17, approved to start 2026-11-18), with no new route, key or field view. (a) A refused job list (403: a suspended company, a removed member) takes every property address and customer off screen until a list is answered again, whichever list or screen asked: list rows, the Restore notice, unfinished archives, a job's cached details and the sync sheet's job labels (`pending.listDenied` in `src/lib/field/pending.ts`). (b) An archive with no answer, or one answered 5xx, may have committed in the job's record with only its field step left: the same request is kept to send again, and the job's screens are read only (every section, the estimator picker and photo sends included) until a read of the job answers (`pending.verify`, archive only: a missing photo does not lock the screen). (c) 410 counts as archived by its status, with or without the body's `archived` code, in the Archive answer and the job read. (d) A job found archived keeps what the person changed and did not save, read only: open forms, an offline prep-task Remove, a room name typed and not added, and an own prep task typed and not added; a job opened from this phone's saved field work whose forms hold such changes stays mounted instead of showing the notice alone. (e) The Copy browser test (`web/tests/e2e/archive-jobs.spec.ts`) goes through the real copy route from a signed snapshot fixture: the first real answer is dropped, the retry sends the same ids and is answered 200, and one draft holds the copied scope.
