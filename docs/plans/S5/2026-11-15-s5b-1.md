# S5 plan correction: 2026-11-15, S5b-1

Part of [the S5 plan](../S5.md), section 12, "Corrections".

- **2026-11-15, S5b-1.** Office jobs, assignment and the field owner (`web/src/lib/jobs/office.ts`, migration `0015_office_jobs`, the Job coordination view of `/field` and the Start field work step on Check Scope and Photos; `docs/access/jobs.md` "Office jobs routes") add one capability key, `field_owner_transfer` (owner only, J5), with these choices the plan left open. (a) Office "creates an empty job" through the existing `POST /api/jobs`, whose body holds no scope; office goes on to Job coordination after the create, field staff to Check Scope. (b) Assignment rows are made from a client-made id with a stored request digest; unassigning ends the row (`ended_at`, `ended_by_actor_id`), never deletes it, and a trigger freezes an ended row. Only an active estimator of the company may be assigned. The estimator who owns the job's field work cannot be unassigned until the owner moves the field work (409 `assigned_field_owner`): otherwise they would keep the job as its field owner while the office saw them unassigned. (c) An estimator sees the jobs they are actively assigned (the one job-access function, and `scope=mine`), but changes the field work only once they own it: a new photo, Submit and Back to editing need an estimator to be the field owner (403 `not_field_owner`); a photo's full-size original and a signature do not. The owner keeps the access the role gives. (d) "First capture makes me field owner" is an explicit Start field work step (`POST /api/jobs/{id}/field-owner`) before the phone reads the draft, since the field rules let an estimator read only a draft they own. Postgres decides who owns the field work; the draft's `fieldOwnerUid` is set after the Postgres commit in a Firestore transaction with `fieldOwnerRevision` (the job revision), so a slower request never puts back an older owner; a draft step that does not get through answers 503 and the same request again sets it. The field rules are unchanged: they already let a client change only `field`. A signed job refuses a first capture and a move (409 `job_signed`). (e) Job coordination is its own route (`GET`/`PUT /api/jobs/{id}/coordination`, `coordination` or `assignment` to read, `coordination` to save the notes), so `GET /api/jobs/{id}` stays the customer view for everyone; office notes are coordination, not the signed record, so a signed job keeps them. (f) "Office-owned customer details" in `docs/spec.md` 6.2 are read as the job coordination: owner decision J3 lets the field owner set the customer's name, phone, email and the address while the job is unsigned. (g) `agreement_corrections` is insert-only (`SELECT`, `INSERT`), one row per corrected field (`property_address`, `customer_name`, `customer_phone`, `customer_email`) beside an agreement of the same job; S5b-4 adds its routes and documents.
