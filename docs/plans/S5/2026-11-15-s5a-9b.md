# S5 plan correction: 2026-11-15, S5a-9b

Part of [the S5 plan](../S5.md), section 12, "Corrections".

- **2026-11-15, S5a-9b.** The proposal layouts (`web/src/engine/proposal-letter.ts`, `makeDocuments`'s `settings` and `rooms` inputs, `web/src/lib/estimates/proposal.ts`, the review's and the signed page's `Proposal`) use no migration and no new capability, with these choices the plan left open. (a) `readCompanyDocuments` returns the stored letterhead fields, the layout and the company's clauses; the photo log stays grouped by room until S5a-9c. (b) The proposal keeps format `dryscope-proposal-v2` and adds keys only when the company set them: `company_clauses` (either layout) and `letter` (in place of the itemized `findings` and `recommendations`, so the signed bytes hold only what the customer is shown). A company with no settings gets S5a-9's bytes exactly (tested against fixed SHA-256 values), and stored v2 proposals still read. (c) The letter's sections: `area_inspected` (every room by name), `findings` (the age sentence) and `room_findings` (one `{room, text}` per room with a note, exactly as recorded), `recommendations` (testing before 1978, then protective equipment), `room_work` (per room with prep tasks or lines: each prep task as "* {task}", then each line as "{item}: up to {quantity} {unit}", {item} being the price-list item's customer description or name and {unit} its unit as stored, such as "sf" or "each"), `job_work` (lines priced for the whole job rather than a room, the job charges such as a service call included, after the rooms and in the same form, so nothing priced is left off a signed letter; approved additional work keeps the engine's own sentence) and `additional` (the mandated sentence on additional affected materials, which also ends the itemized scope list). (d) With `show_line_breakdown` on, each letter line carries its `total`; the letter's price is its one price line (the price or range and its explanation), with no `price.lines` and no charge rows. The itemized proposal's bytes are unchanged. With it off, either layout shows the work without prices and only the price line: the review and the signed page print no lines and no charge rows (subtotal, after-hours, trade minimums, minimum charge, overhead and profit, tax), a rendering change only. (e) The company's clauses print after the recommendations and before the standard clauses in both layouts, title and text exactly as entered, line breaks kept. (f) Company settings' layout explanations now say what each layout prints.
