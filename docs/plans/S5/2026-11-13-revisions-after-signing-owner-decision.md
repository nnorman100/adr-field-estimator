# S5 plan correction: 2026-11-13, revisions after signing (owner decision)

Part of [the S5 plan](../S5.md), section 12, "Corrections".

- **2026-11-13, revisions after signing (owner decision).** The owner's requirement is that any change becomes a revised estimate needing a new signature. `docs/spec.md` 6.1 (Q5, "Change orders after signing") allows a change order for positive added work only and never edits or reprices the original; both rules still hold, but they left no path for removing or reducing signed work. So: after signing, any change (adding, removing or reducing a room, item or quantity) can be made as a revised estimate (S5b-9, kind `revision` added in S5b-8's migration) that supersedes the signed agreement without changing it, prints "Revised {date}: was {signed price}" (S5b-6) and applies only once the customer signs it; a change order stays the in-progress path for added work and is signed too. S5a-11, S5b-6, S5b-8 and S5b-9 carry this. S5a-9b no longer labels a room with no lines "checked; no work proposed": a room with no lines can still have proposed work the engine could not price (for example a Mold room without a condition), so Area inspected lists each room by name with its recorded findings only (S5a-9b).
