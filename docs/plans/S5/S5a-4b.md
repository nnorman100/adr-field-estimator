# S5 plan, step S5a-4b

Part of [the S5 plan](../S5.md), section 5, "Ordered steps".

**S5a-4b Letterhead fields and document settings (DB job; added 2026-11-13, D4).** Every company sets these for itself; a new company starts with every field empty and every setting at the neutral default shown, and no company's own values are seeded. DB: on `companies`, the letterhead columns `profile_emergency_phone`, `profile_fax`, `profile_website`, `profile_tagline` and `profile_mailing_address` (text, empty until set, length checks) and `profile_documents` (`jsonb`, an object under 64 KiB; its shape is one Zod schema checked at write and at read, as price versions are). The column-level `UPDATE` grant and `0010`'s trigger split extend to the new columns (a company's own transaction changes them; an operator never does). `profile_documents` holds: `proposal_layout` (`itemized`, the default and today's layout, or `letter`; S5a-9b); `company_clauses` (up to 10, each a title and up to 2,000 characters, printed exactly as entered after the recommendations and before the standard clauses; S5a-9b); `prep_tasks` (up to 30 templates of up to 300 characters, such as "The homeowner removes the contents of the closet before the start date"; S5a-10, S5a-9b); `photo_log_grouping` (`room`, the default, or `shot_tag`; S5a-9c); `authorization_text` and `payment_terms_text` (up to 4,000 characters each, the start-work authorization and payment terms printed on the signed page; S5a-11); `show_previous_price` (off by default; S5b-6). Line prices on the proposal are shown or hidden by the price table's existing `show_line_breakdown` setting (S5a-9b), so that setting is not repeated here. UI: Company settings gains the letterhead fields and a Documents section for these settings, each with a plain example. BE: the S5a-4 profile routes read and save them (owner only, `profile_manage`, the same revision and `changeId` rule); `companySetupMissing` lists empty `authorization_text` or `payment_terms_text` as missing before signing (S5a-11). Tests: guard test; the owner saves and reads each field; estimator, office and operator refused; an unknown key, an oversized clause or list, and a layout outside the list are refused; company B never reads or changes A's settings; the migration applies on a database holding every earlier file. Needs: S5a-4, the lane.
