# Billing plan, step SB-15

Part of [the billing plan](../BILLING.md), section 5, "Ordered steps".

**SB-15 Recording who was active (S, High: touches `/api/me`).** Slice: server.
- BE: `GET /api/me` records the caller in `member_activity` for the UTC month of the open's own time (section 4.5), never for a period stored by the sync: one upsert that sets the first open's time once and the latest open's time on every open. A failure to record never fails `/api/me`.
- Tests: two opens in a month make one row and a third in the next month makes a second; an open at 09:00 and one at 13:00 leave 09:00 as the first and 13:00 as the latest; an open at 00:01 on the 1st, before the sync has seen the new period, is recorded in the new month; a disabled member, an operator's support view, a signing link and a refused sign-in record nothing; a person in two companies is recorded once in each; `/api/me` still answers when the insert fails; the answer's shape is unchanged.
- Needs: SB-14.
