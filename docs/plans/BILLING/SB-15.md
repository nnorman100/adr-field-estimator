# Billing plan, step SB-15

Part of [the billing plan](../BILLING.md), section 5, "Ordered steps".

**SB-15 Recording who was active (S, High: touches `/api/me`).** Slice: server.
- BE: `GET /api/me` records the caller in `member_activity` for the company's current period (section 4.5: the start instant on `company_billing`, or the UTC month when there is none), inserting the first open's time once and the latest day at most once a day, skipping the write when this server instance has already recorded that person, company, period and day. A failure to record never fails `/api/me`.
- Tests: two opens in a month make one row and a third in the next month makes a second; an open at 09:00, a subscription start at 12:00 and an open at 13:00 make two rows, the second with 13:00 as its first open, even on the same server instance; a disabled member, an operator's support view, a signing link and a refused sign-in record nothing; a person in two companies is recorded once in each; `/api/me` still answers when the insert fails; the answer's shape is unchanged.
- Needs: SB-14.
