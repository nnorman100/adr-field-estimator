# Billing plan, step SB-13

Part of [the billing plan](../BILLING.md), section 5, "Ordered steps".

**SB-13 Read-only on the phone and the screens (M, High: sync state).** Slice: screens.
- UI: one line on every field and office screen of a read-only company: "Read-only until billing is sorted out. You can still open and download everything." The owner's version adds the pay or export button. Create, edit, sign, photo and AI controls are disabled, each with the reason.
- FE: the sync gate gains a read-only verdict: unsent edits and photos stay on the phone, nothing is sent, and they send when `/api/me` says `full` again; the photo queue treats `billing_read_only` as waiting. A write the rules refuse makes the phone ask `/api/me` at once (today it asks again only shortly before the access record expires), so an open phone moves to read-only before the journal can treat the refusal as final. Reading uses what the phone already holds plus the server's read routes.
- Tests: Playwright on Chromium and WebKit at 390 px with axe, with the offline harness of S6-1: a phone with two unsent edits and one unsent photo goes read-only, shows them as kept, sends nothing; access returns and each arrives exactly once; a phone that was open and online when the company became read-only keeps an edit made a moment later; no control on any screen can start a write in read-only (the test taps each); the journal never marks a read-only refusal as lost.
- Needs: SB-9, SB-12; S6-4 and S6-5 (merged). SB-12b follows it.
