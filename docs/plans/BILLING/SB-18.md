# Billing plan, step SB-18

Part of [the billing plan](../BILLING.md), section 5, "Ordered steps".

**SB-18 Reporting use to Stripe and closing the month (L, High: what a customer is charged).** Slice: server.
- BE: in the billing-sync route: the hourly report of the three totals for each subscribed company (section 4.6); on `invoice.created` the wait for calls and sessions still in progress, the month's freeze, the final report stamped inside the month, and the read-back check; an alert when Stripe's totals and the frozen row differ; nothing sent for a company that is `manual`, read-only for the whole month, or has no subscription.
- Docs: `docs/runbook/billing-operations.md` (the month end, a difference, a late correction by hand).
- Tests, against the stand-in with a movable clock: the invoice quantity equals the frozen row for a month of planted use; a report sent three times bills once; a crash after sending and before recording (killed child) is repeated harmlessly; a total that falls is the new last value; the final report's stamp is inside the month; a call that started before midnight and settled after it is billed in the month it started; a company that subscribed on the 15th is billed nothing for the 1st to the 14th; a difference raises the alert and writes the read-back columns while the frozen figures stay unchanged; a month is never re-sent after its window; company A's totals are never sent with company B's customer id.
- Needs: SB-5, SB-16, SB-0's results. B22.
