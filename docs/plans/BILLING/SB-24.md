# Billing plan, step SB-24

Part of [the billing plan](../BILLING.md), section 5, "Ordered steps".

**SB-24 Cancellation and the closing clock (M, High).** Slice: server.
- BE: the sync sets the closing date when a subscription ends; a returning company (a new subscription) clears it; the operator's list route answers companies past the date; closing itself is the existing suspend and archive (B18). Nothing is deleted.
- Tests: an ended subscription sets read-only and a date 30 days on; a new subscription before the date restores `full`; a company never closes by itself; a `manual` company never gets a date.
- Needs: SB-5. B13, B18.
