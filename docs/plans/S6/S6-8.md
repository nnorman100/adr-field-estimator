# S6 plan, step S6-8

Part of [the S6 plan](../S6.md), section 5, "Ordered steps".

**S6-8 Dated read-only item list on the phone (S).** UI: Check Scope offers the item list and room types with no signal, labelled "Item list as of {date}"; no price or total is ever shown from the phone copy. FE: `ds-prices`, refreshed on open when online and when a newer published version exists, keyed by company and version id. BE: a route (or a field of S4-6's read) that returns only item ids, names, units, room types and suggestion lists for the company's published version, no prices. DB, FS: none. Tests: Check Scope lists items offline; a copy older than the published version says so; a company with no published prices shows "prices not set"; submitting an item id the pinned version lacks is refused by the server with the items listed (S5a-10), and the phone shows them. Needs: S4-6, S5a-10 (and S6-3). It is in the pricing plan's list of S6 work ("the phone gets the published table, dated, read-only") and is not on the Standard's list; it can close after S6-9 without holding it.
