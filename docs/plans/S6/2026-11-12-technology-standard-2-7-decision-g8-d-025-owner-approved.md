# S6 plan correction: 2026-11-12, Technology Standard 2.7 decision G8 (D-025), owner-approved

Part of [the S6 plan](../S6.md), section 12, "Corrections".

- **2026-11-12, Technology Standard 2.7 decision G8 ([D-025](../../DECISIONS.md)), owner-approved.** Supersedes O1's "ask, do not require". On iPhone, offline capture requires the app installed to the Home Screen; in a Safari tab the field screens work online only and say why. The app asks for persistent storage (`persist()`) at the first save. Effect on the steps: S6-2's first-run sheet tells an iPhone user in a Safari tab that offline work needs the installed app; S6-5's browser-tab banner and 3-day warning give way to the online-only notice, since a Safari tab keeps no unsent work. Read section 1 ("What iOS decides"), O1 in section 8, the section 7 iPhone check and the section 11 Safari row with this entry.
