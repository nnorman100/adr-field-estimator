# S6 plan correction: 2026-11-18: S6-4b screens, and photos in Save as new draft

Part of [the S6 plan](../S6.md), section 12, "Corrections".

S6-4b builds the screens of [S6-4](S6-4.md) on the merged recovery journal (S6-4a): the sync chip on every field screen, the "Can't save" sheet, and Save as new draft, Discard and Try again (`web/src/app/field/sync-chip.tsx`, `web/src/lib/field/sync-status.ts`, `web/src/lib/field/new-draft.ts`). Where it differs from the step's paragraph:

- **Photos (Firstmate decision A, 2026-11-18).** The photo queue `ds-queue` is S6-5's and does not exist yet, so there are no queued `photo` entries to re-point and no photo bytes on the phone after a restart. Save as new draft copies the cached draft's field map and rooms with the refused edits on top, and no photo references: a photo id is registered to its job and cannot be used under another one. The sheet says that photos stay with the old job. **S6-5 re-points queued photos** to the new draft, with new photo ids chosen before the first send, when it builds the queue.
- **Archived or finished.** Both come from the server's copy of the draft when the edit is refused, never from the phone's cache: a draft archived while it was being edited is "This job was archived"; one being finished or finished, also when that job was archived after (the archive keeps the fence), is "This job was finished", which offers no Try again and no Save as new draft: the sheet links to Finish and review, where Back to editing is (J4), for the job the screen that made the edit read.
- **New ids.** The new job's and draft's ids are derived from the person and the draft the edits were refused for (a SHA-256 of the tenant, the person and the draft's path), so they are chosen before the first send and need nothing more stored: every try for that draft, also after a lost answer or a later refusal, gives the same ids and one job. The new job's property address is empty (the address and customer belong to Postgres; the person adds them on the new job), and the copy leaves out `scopeConfirmed`, as S5b-2's Copy does. Before the copy, the server is asked again (the person's access, the draft, and the job); a room order that names a room the phone does not hold copies nothing. The copy is made once: once the new draft holds field work, only refused edits not yet copied are written to it, never the cached draft again.
- **Try again** asks the server for the person's access now (it must let them in), the draft, and the job (a 410 is an archive the draft does not show yet). Still refused: the entries keep the reason the server now gives. Not any more (restored, field work moved back, access or role given back): in one transaction that reads the documents the edits write, they are sent again, in the order they were made, only while the server still holds what the phone held when each was made (kept with each edit); otherwise they are kept as "Changed on another device", never sent over newer work.
- **Online only.** With a cache that keeps nothing on the phone, the chip says the same three words from the edits' own answers, held in the page's memory, and the sheet offers the same actions; nothing is kept after the page closes.
