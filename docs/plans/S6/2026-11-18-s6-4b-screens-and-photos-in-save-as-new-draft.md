# S6 plan correction: 2026-11-18: S6-4b screens, and photos in Save as new draft

Part of [the S6 plan](../S6.md), section 12, "Corrections".

S6-4b builds the screens of [S6-4](S6-4.md) on the merged recovery journal (S6-4a): the sync chip on every field screen, the "Can't save" sheet, and Save as new draft, Discard and Try again (`web/src/app/field/sync-chip.tsx`, `web/src/lib/field/sync-status.ts`, `web/src/lib/field/new-draft.ts`). Where it differs from the step's paragraph:

- **Photos (Firstmate decision A, 2026-11-18).** The photo queue `ds-queue` is S6-5's and does not exist yet, so there are no queued `photo` entries to re-point and no photo bytes on the phone after a restart. Save as new draft copies the cached draft's field map and rooms with the refused edits on top, and no photo references: a photo id is registered to its job and cannot be used under another one. The sheet says that photos stay with the old job. **S6-5 re-points queued photos** to the new draft, with new photo ids chosen before the first send, when it builds the queue.
- **Archived or finished.** Both come from the server: its copy of the draft and, where that leaves it open, the jobs API (a 410 is an archive that has not reached the draft yet), judged the same way when the edit is refused, in recovery after a restart (before any edit is made again), each time the sync gate opens (for a refusal whose reason was not read yet) and before each action; never from the phone's cache. A draft archived while it was being edited is "This job was archived"; one being finished or finished, also when that job was archived after (the archive keeps the fence), is "This job was finished", which offers no Try again and no Save as new draft. A draft an estimator can no longer read moved to someone else only while the job is still being edited on that draft; finished since, it is finished, and otherwise (archived, not shown to them) it is no access, so nothing unreadable is copied on a guess; Save as new draft is offered only with the job the edits were made on known. For a finished job the sheet links to Finish and review, where Back to editing is (J4), for the job the screen that made the edit read, once the job answers; when it answers 410 the sheet says the company owner must restore it first. Only a 410 from the jobs API is an archived job.
- **The chip and the sheet.** The chip says the most urgent reason ("Can't save: no access", then role changed, field work moved, archived, finished, checking why), never a count of jobs. The sheet lists the refused and changed edits by job and reason, with no waiting count of its own.
- **New ids.** The new job's and draft's ids are derived from the person and the draft the edits were refused for (a SHA-256 of the tenant, the person and the draft's path), so they are chosen before the first send and need nothing more stored: every try for that draft, also after a lost answer, gives the same ids and one job. The new job's property address is empty (the address and customer belong to Postgres; the person adds them on the new job), and the copy keeps every cached value, `scopeConfirmed` too. Before the copy, the server is asked again (the person's access, the draft, and the job); a room order that names a room the phone does not hold copies nothing. The copy is written in one transaction, only into a new draft still as POST /api/jobs made it (an empty field map, none of the rooms it makes); a new draft that already holds the copy is saved as it is.
- **One copy per refused draft (simplification, Firstmate, review round 3).** A copy is never merged into. A later refusal of the same draft, once its new draft holds anything else (the first copy, or work made on it since), offers **Save as another new draft**: a separate new job and draft whose ids also come from the refused edits' own ids, so each try of those edits makes one job too, or Discard. The phone keeps no record of what a copy wrote, and the "Changed on another device" rows that the merge made are gone with it.
- **Try again** asks the server for the person's access now (it must let them in), the draft, and the job (a 410 is an archive the draft does not show yet). Still refused: the entries keep the reason the server now gives. Not any more (restored, field work moved back, access or role given back): in one transaction that reads the documents the edits write, they are sent again, in the order they were made, only while the server still holds what the screen showed as it built each (kept with each edit); otherwise they are kept as "Changed on another device", never sent over newer work. Once sent, the job's open screen reads the job and its draft again, and a photo whose reference was sent leaves the photos being sent. Photos' Send again of a reference the server refused sends that same journal entry the same way, never a second one, and Discard of it in the sheet takes the photo out of the photos being sent too.
- **What the sheet shows.** A refusal is recorded on the phone as soon as Firestore gives it, before its reason is read, so a page closed meanwhile never sends it again. An entry rejected before its job was kept (S6-4a) is classified again, and keeps the job the server's draft names. Once the server refuses the person the job (no access), the sheet shows nothing of the job (no address). "Changed on another device" lists each changed field by name. Finish and review is asked about each time the sheet opens. Discard says it removes the changes from this phone and leaves what DryScope has; the sheet says the changes stay on the phone only when they do (not in Online only).
- **Online only.** With a cache that keeps nothing on the phone, the chip says the same three words from the edits' own answers, held in the page's memory, and the sheet offers the same actions; nothing is kept after the page closes, so leaving the page asks first while any edit is waiting, refused or changed.
- **Sign-in.** The chip and the sheet are part of the signed-in person's screens: they close with them when that person signs out or another signs in.
