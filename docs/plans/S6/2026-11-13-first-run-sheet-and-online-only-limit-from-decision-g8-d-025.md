# S6 plan correction: 2026-11-13: first-run sheet and online-only limit, from decision G8 (D-025)

Part of [the S6 plan](../S6.md), section 12, "Corrections".

Closes the "not done here" line above for the shell. The owner re-confirmed G8 as "Option A" on 2026-11-13 and was not familiar with the phrase "add to Home Screen", so the sheet explains it.

- **Where it applies.** `web/src/lib/field/platform.ts` tells the page where it runs: `ios-tab` (an iPhone or iPad browser tab, including an iPad that asks for the desktop site), `ios-icon` (opened from the Home Screen icon: `display-mode: standalone` or `navigator.standalone`) or `other` (Android, computers). `offlineCaptureAllowed` is false only for `ios-tab`, and until the page knows (the server render and the first client render). The shell root carries `data-offline-capture="allowed|blocked"`; the capture screens of S5a-6 and S6-5 must ask `offlineCaptureAllowed`, never decide for themselves.
- **In an iPhone tab.** The first-run sheet is now "Save DryScope to your Home Screen": what the Home Screen is, why (Safari erases a website's saved data, unsent photos and notes included, after about a week without a visit; a Home Screen website is exempt), four plain steps (Share button, Add to Home Screen, Add, open DryScope from the new icon and sign in there) and the help line. It opens once, then from a button that sits with a standing notice. With no signal the banner says "in Safari, DryScope works online only" instead of "your work is saved on this phone", which a tab cannot promise. Online use is unchanged. The wording never says "install" or names a store.
- **Elsewhere.** Opened from the Home Screen icon, nothing is asked. On Android and computers nothing is asked and nothing is limited; they no longer get the Safari-only sheet. S6-5's browser-tab banner and 3-day warning stay dropped (a tab keeps no unsent work).
- **Tests.** `web/tests/unit/field-platform.test.ts`; `web/tests/e2e/offline/home-screen.spec.ts` (iPhone tab, iPhone from the icon by both signals, Android, computer; Chromium and WebKit; the network cut at the lane). `field-shell.spec.ts` now runs the sheet test under an iPhone identity and expects no sheet on a computer; its sign-in steps lost a "Got it" click that only dismissed the old sheet on a computer.
- **Not covered.** No real iPhone: the tests use iPhone identities in desktop Chromium and WebKit (Linux). The owner's phone check is below.
