How a job runs

Three steps, one visit

  1. 1

    Walk & Photo

    Start the live walkthrough and talk through each room while the camera streams. The assistant listens, watches and asks for the shots an adjuster expects: room overview, each affected wall or floor, a meter reading on every wet material, the source of loss, contents and equipment as placed.

  2. 2

    Check Scope

    Fill the scope from what was said, then confirm it. Each room carries its water category and a Water, Mold or Water + Mold loss type, measured demolition areas, waste and cabinetry; equipment counts and drying days price from the estimating sheet's rates.

  3. 3

    Review & Sign

    Review the customer estimate range beside the itemized base-rate total. The homeowner signs on the technician's phone, or on their own through a private link that works once, expires in 7 days and dies on any later edit.

Questions buyers ask

Straight answers before you ask for a demo

  • What is DryScope?

    A web app a restoration technician opens on a phone at a water or mold loss. It coaches the walkthrough by voice and camera, fills the scope from what the technician says, and produces a priced proposal, an itemized claim, a scope defense letter and a photo moisture log from one inspection.

  • Who is it for?

    Owners and operations managers of water mitigation and disaster restoration companies, and the estimators, technicians and office staff who work their jobs. Each company works in its own isolated workspace with its own branding on every document.

  • How does a company get access?

    By invitation only. There is no public sign-up: a company is created for its owner, the owner invites estimators and office staff by email, and each person sets a password from the invitation or signs in with Google. Homeowners never need an account; they review and sign through a private link.

  • Which documents does it produce?

    Four from one inspection: a proposal with a low–high customer estimate ready for an e-signature, an itemized claim with every line's quantity, rate, waste and total, an IICRC S500 / S520 scope defense letter addressed to the recorded adjuster, and a photo moisture log with each photo's room, time and recognized meter reading.

  • Does the AI set the prices?

    No. Every price is computed on the server from a cell-traced estimating sheet, in exact decimals, together with the mandatory wording. The model drafts the narrative and fills the form from the technician's words; it never supplies a price, overrides a total or removes a required clause, and work the sheet does not price blocks signing until a technician corrects it.

  • Which losses does it estimate?

    Water and mold losses only. Fire, smoke and other loss types are out of scope.

  • Does it work in the field without signal?

    It is an installable web app for any phone or tablet browser. Drafts save on the device first and sync when online, and offline notes keep voice notes, photos and typed notes on the phone until they upload. The live assistant, document generation and signing need a connection.

Built for the field

What the crew gets on the phone

  • Live voice-and-camera walkthrough

    Real-time voice coaching while the phone camera streams the loss. Weak signal pauses video and keeps the voice going.

  • Required photo shot list

    Per-room missing shots, one-tap tagging after each capture, and a count of what is still missing before documents are generated.

  • Drying log with psychrometrics

    Temperature and humidity become grains per pound and dew point as they are typed; material and thermal readings keep their dry standard and ΔT, per room and per day.

  • QR room and equipment tags

    Print tags for each room and each air mover, dehumidifier or scrubber. A scan selects the room or records where the unit stands.

  • IICRC equipment-sizing check

    Recommended air movers and LGR dehumidification from floor area, ceiling height and water class, flagged Above, Below or Within range beside what is priced.

  • Claim & loss details

    Carrier, claim and policy numbers, date and cause of loss, adjuster contact and deductible, printed into the documents.

  • Per-room water or mold loss type

    Rooms are tagged WATER, MOLD or both, with the category and cabinetry rules the sheet applies to each.

  • Your company's branding

    Each company is an isolated workspace. Your name, logo, license and contact details head every document; DryScope stays behind the scenes.
