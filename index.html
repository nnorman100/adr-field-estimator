        For your role

        Getting started

        Signing in and getting around
        • Tap Sign in for a Google account, or enter your email and password. New staff set a password with the link in their invitation email.
        • A new email account must be verified: open the link in the verification email, then tap I've verified.
        • Invited to a company? Accept the invitation on your dashboard with the same email address it was sent to.
        • On a phone, Menu lists your dashboard screens. Account has sign-out and the company contact details. Inside a job, tap ← Jobs to return to your jobs; More holds Job coordination, New inspection, Save draft, Help and Account.
        • Offline, changes stay on this device until they sync. The AI assistant, documents and signing need a connection.
        • When Update ready appears, tap Save & update. Your drafts are kept.

        App features

        My jobs and Company jobs
        • Estimators see My jobs: the jobs they created or are assigned. Owners and office staff see all Company jobs.
        • Tap a job to open its draft or signed agreement. Use Load older drafts or Load older agreements for more.
        • + New job at the top of the dashboard starts an inspection. Inside a job, Jobs (More on a phone) also opens drafts and signed jobs or starts a new inspection.
        Property and scope
        • The steps run left to right: 1. Walk & Photo, 2. Check Scope, 3. Review & Sign. A new job opens on Walk & Photo; tap Check Scope to type a desk estimate instead.
        • On Check Scope, enter the address and confirmed year built, then Add room for each affected room with its water category and measured square feet. Each room card lists its photos.
        • Tap Water, Mold or Water + Mold on each room; mold sets category 3. Waste % and cabinetry wait under More room options. Enter air movers, dehumidifiers and drying days, confirm Standard job charges, and open More equipment & labor for wood-floor drying, added labor and after-hours losses.
        • Describe crawl spaces, countertops, water heaters and other conditions in the notes.
        • Check the scope confirmation box once every quantity is right. Drafts save automatically and the status under the job title reads Saved, Saving… or Offline · saved on phone; Jobs (More on a phone) → Save draft saves now.
        Walkthrough with the live camera assistant
        • On Walk & Photo, tap a room chip under Current room to select it, tap the selected chip to rename it, or tap + Room to add one. Then tap Start walkthrough and allow the camera and microphone.
        • Speak naturally as you inspect. The assistant listens, watches the camera and talks back. Its replies are guidance, not observed evidence.
        • Tap Capture photo for a photo of the current room: it grabs a frame while Live runs and opens the camera otherwise. Upload file adds an existing photo. Change the room chip as you move.
        • Mute microphone pauses listening; Stop walkthrough ends the session. If the connection drops, tap Resume walkthrough.
        • Color emulation and the walkthrough transcript are under More walkthrough options. The emulation is a visual filter, not thermal evidence.
        • Fill scope from walkthrough fills quote-backed form values from what you said. Review fields marked From walkthrough; Undo restores prior values. Then confirm scope.
        • Bad reception? Tap Offline notes, or Switch to offline notes when the app offers it. Record voice notes, take photos and type notes per room; they stay on the phone. Once online, voice notes become transcript text, the scope is filled for review and anything still missing is listed. Tap Back to live assistant when reception returns.
        Photo evidence
        • The photo strip on Walk & Photo lists every photo with its room, capture time and recognition status; each room card on Check Scope shows its own photos. Use the room menu on a photo to move it to another room.
        • Moisture-meter readings in photos are read automatically. Pending or failed readings stay unverified; tap Retry recognition to try again.
        • Original photos are kept privately with the job.
        Review and claim documents
        • When the scope is confirmed, the estimator taps Finish & review. It stops capture and builds four documents from the saved inspection: Proposal, Itemized claim, Defense letter and Photo audit log.
        • Open all four before signing. The proposal shows the customer estimate range; the itemized charges at base rates appear beside it.
        • Unpriced work or pending photo recognition blocks signing. Any edit means the documents must be generated again.
        • Export copies or downloads the scope JSON, or prints the documents.
        Getting the homeowner's signature
        • In person: review the documents with the homeowner, check the confirmation box, have them sign the pad and tap Authorize & sign.
        • On their own phone: tap Send to homeowner, then show the QR code, copy the link or share it. The homeowner needs no account.
        • The link works once, expires in 7 days and stops working if anything in the inspection changes. Revoke link cancels it.
        • Review switches to the signed agreement when the homeowner signs. The app sends no email or text.
        Job coordination and assigning estimators
        • Office staff can tap New job to start a job without rooms or measurements; the estimator adds the scope.
        • Open a job from Company jobs. In Job coordination (on a phone, open it from More), set the property address, Assigned estimator, customer contact and office notes, then tap Save coordination.
        • The assigned estimator sees the job in My jobs.
        • Office staff can view and export documents but cannot change the scope, capture photos or sign.
        Team and invitations
        • Open Team & invitations. Under Invite staff, enter an email, choose Estimator or Office staff and tap Send invitation.
        • Invitations last 7 days. The person accepts after signing in with that exact email address.
        • For each member, change the role or disable access and tap Save access. Resend or cancel pending invitations from the list.
        Company settings
        • Open Company settings to edit the company name, address, phone, contact email, CSLB license, document sign-off and optional logo (PNG or JPEG, up to 1 MB).
        • These details appear on new proposals. After a change, generate unsigned documents again before signing.
        Companies and the support view
        • Open Companies. Under Create a company, enter the name and owner's email and tap Create & invite owner.
        • Manage company suspends or reactivates a company and invites or replaces its owner.
        • Open read-only support view shows the company's jobs, documents, photos, team and invitations. Nothing there can be edited.
        • The support view allows only a few fixes: change a member between estimator and office staff, re-enable access, and resend or cancel an invitation. Every view and action is recorded in the audit log.

        For company access, ask your company owner.
