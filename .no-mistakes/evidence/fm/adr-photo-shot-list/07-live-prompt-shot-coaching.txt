- Room overview: a wide photo of the whole room
- Source of loss: the source of the water or moisture (only when the room is marked "Source of loss is here")
- Affected wall or floor: each affected wall, floor or ceiling surface
- Meter reading: a readable meter on each wet material, held on that surface
- Contents before moving: the contents in place before anything is moved (only when the room is marked "Contents in room")
- Equipment as placed: the drying equipment as placed (only when the room is marked "Equipment placed here")
