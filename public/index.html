Questions owners ask

  • What is DryScope?

    A web app a restoration technician opens on a phone at a water or mold loss. It coaches the walkthrough by voice and camera, fills the scope from what the technician says, and produces a priced proposal, an itemized claim, a scope defense letter and a photo moisture log from one inspection.

  • Who is it for?

    Owners and operations managers of water mitigation and disaster restoration companies, and the estimators, technicians and office staff who work their jobs. Each company works in its own isolated workspace with its own branding on every document.

  • How does a company get access?

    By invitation only; there is no public sign-up. A company is created for its owner, the owner invites estimators and office staff by email, and each person sets a password from the invitation or signs in with Google. Homeowners never need an account: they review and sign through a private link.

  • Which documents does it produce?

    Four from one inspection: a proposal with a low–high customer estimate, an itemized claim, an IICRC S500 / S520 scope defense letter addressed to the recorded adjuster, and a photo moisture log with each photo's room, time and recognized meter reading.

  • Does the AI set the prices?

    No. Every price is computed on the server from a cell-traced estimating sheet, together with the mandatory wording. The model drafts the narrative and fills the form from the technician's words; it never supplies a price, overrides a total or removes a required clause. Work the sheet does not price is flagged as unpriced and blocks signing until a technician corrects it.

  • Which losses does it estimate?

    Water and mold losses only. Fire, smoke and other loss types are out of scope.

  • Does it work in the field without signal?

    It is an installable web app for any phone or tablet browser. Drafts save on the device first and sync when online, and offline notes keep voice notes, photos and typed notes on the phone until they upload. The live assistant, document generation and signing need a connection.
